# Future Sky — Trajectory

**Version:** 2.2  
**Status:** Living constitutional direction  
**Reconciled:** 2026-08-31  
**Supersedes:** `TRAJECTORY_v2.1.md`

## Purpose

Future Sky is not being built from mechanics upward.

Nor is it being built from stories downward.

It is being built through the emergence of civilisation itself.

Mechanics, stories, institutions, cultures and technologies are expressions of a deeper process:

**Consciousness becoming relationship.  
Relationship becoming community.  
Community becoming civilisation.  
Civilisation attempting to remember itself through the continual relationship between consciousness, memory, time and meaning.**

Future Sky does not seek to simulate every possibility.

**It seeks to construct a world in which meaningful possibilities can continuously emerge.**

This document answers:

> **What is Future Sky becoming?**

It is directional rather than operational. Because direction responds to what the world becomes capable of, TRAJECTORY is living and versioned—not frozen canon.

## Authority and change

TRAJECTORY carries constitutional weight when evaluating development direction. It does not override the Red Book on world canon, the reconciled ontology on fundamental concepts, or runtime evidence on what is implemented.

Its authority is therefore:

- stronger than a temporary roadmap or implementation convenience;
- weaker than a permanent Natural Law or explicit constitutional prohibition;
- open to deliberate revision as Future Sky learns what it is becoming.

Changes should preserve lineage. A superseded trajectory remains part of project history rather than becoming an embarrassment to erase.

## Core premise

Future Sky is a simulation of a civilisation whose primary medium is meaning rather than mechanics.

Complexity should emerge.

Never be skipped.

Never be faked.

The architecture creates conditions from which living worlds may naturally arise.

## Constitutional invariants

Every future subsystem should preserve these principles:

- Coherence precedes complexity.
- Emergence is preferred over prescription.
- Memory precedes meaning.
- Relationship precedes optimisation.
- Human participation is preferred but never required.
- Observation precedes intervention.
- Stewardship is the mature expression of capability.
- Every Era possesses its own experience of time.
- Systems publish facts before demanding reactions.
- Pattern does not equal meaning.
- Runtime vocabulary does not silently become ontology.
- Continuity must not counterfeit human choice.

## The temporal spine

Future Sky does not contain one universal experience of time.

Different beings may inhabit different temporal realities while sharing one canonical world history.

```text
Moment
  → Memory
  → History
  → Civilisation
  → Myth
  → Cycle
  → Renewal
```

Every Era possesses its own temporal grammar.

- **Jyotzon** experiences time as urgency.
- **Manzo** experiences time as recurrence, fragmentation and rediscovery.
- **Ominicron** experiences time as inheritance and consequence.

Players, Characters, NPCs, Institutions, factions, civilisations, comets and the Cube may experience time differently while inhabiting the same World.

The current runtime implements one canonical World Clock with era and comet contributions resolved into an effective multiplier. This is foundation, not the completion of plural temporal experience.

## Observation, memory and pattern

Future Sky prefers worlds that notice themselves before worlds that constantly change themselves.

The implemented epistemic spine is:

```text
Reality → Event → Observation → History → Pattern
```

This is the current sealed boundary through Feather 20.

- Reality has an identifiable steward.
- Events announce completed facts.
- Observations record that facts were witnessed.
- History preserves events and subscriber outcomes.
- Patterns describe neutral structure across remembered observations.

The next layers must not be assumed merely because the first layers exist.

Relevance, resonance, interpretation and meaning remain open research.

## Chapters, Moments, Scenarios and Thread Nodes

```text
Chapter
  → Moment
  → Scenario
  → Thread Node
```

**Chapters** describe developmental contexts.

**Moments** are emergent coherences of independent conditions. They are not authored directly and are not identical to a clock boundary.

**Scenarios** are bounded living situations that coordinate participants.

**Thread Nodes** are local structures of explicit agency.

The runtime World Opportunity pipeline is scaffolding that may support this emergence. Eligibility and awakening do not automatically create a Moment or activate a Scenario.

## World Moments

World Moments communicate that the World continues independently of the player.

They may include environmental change, rumours, broadcasts, artwork, NPC behaviour, music, Cube resonance, weather and silence.

They communicate life rather than exposition.

An expressive World Moment is distinct from an emergent Moment and from the implemented event called `world_moment.eligible`.

## Human participation

The World always prefers genuine human participation.

The World never depends upon it.

Continuity may be maintained by Human, AI, Script or Dormant Drivers. The Role persists regardless of Driver, but continuity machinery must preserve lineage and must not counterfeit human choice.

## Emergence

Future Sky is designed so authored content is multiplied rather than merely consumed.

Rooms, Roles, stories, Institutions, objects and relationships should participate in many possible futures.

The architecture values meaningful recombination over endless expansion.

Civilisation should gradually become capable of generating history that no author explicitly wrote.

## Stewardship

The highest expression of participation is stewardship.

Ownership asks:

> What belongs to me?

Stewardship asks:

> What has been entrusted to my care?

## Love

LOVE is not a reward.

LOVE is not a mechanic.

LOVE is orientation.

Participants may first encounter LOVE as a mystery, code, phrase or civilisation. Eventually they may discover that LOVE has quietly organised the architecture from the beginning.

It is never enforced.

Only discovered.

Love must not be converted into a score simply because the system can now remember and recognise patterns.

## Current developmental position

Feathers 1–20 have established a vertical proof that the World can:

- maintain canonical time;
- observe the real sky;
- derive authored comet truth;
- resolve temporal influence;
- project a world field;
- recognise temporal opportunity boundaries;
- bind context;
- match, gate and persist world opportunities;
- publish immutable facts;
- observe those facts;
- remember completed dispatches;
- query history;
- recognise neutral patterns.

This is substantial infrastructure. It is not yet meaning.

The immediate developmental task is documentary coherence: establish a trusted Knowledge Atlas, authority hierarchy and current working set before extending the runtime beyond Pattern.

## Near horizon

After documentary coherence is sealed, the next research territory is the boundary beneath Pattern:

- what makes a remembered pattern relevant;
- how resonance differs from similarity or frequency;
- whether convergence can be recognised without manufacturing significance;
- how interpretation can remain plural;
- how meaning may emerge without becoming centralised control.

No next feather is authorised by this list alone. Each requires its own bounded architectural question, evidence and explicit sealing decision.
