# Future Sky — Immersive Player Experience

**Status:** Development direction adopted by Karun, 18 October 2026  
**Frame:** MUD 7.0; existing [eight-wing charter](EIGHT_WINGS_CHARTER.md)  
**Current work:** [W2-F3 — The Opening We Can Inhabit](../wings/W2-F3_THE_OPENING_WE_CAN_INHABIT.md)

Put energy into immersive player experience, world-building and the opening sequence. Build greater context through inhabiting the world so that larger engine work can become meaningful.

Larger World Engine expansion is on hold. Preserve existing architecture and running systems. Bounded reliability or delivery work can support a concrete experience when selected; this direction does not authorise a general refactor, deployment or new engine layer.

W2 Worlds is home for the opening exploration. Other wings contribute through linked, bounded work: Mythos for givens and mystery, Beings for identity and relationships, Play for meaningful actions, Time for pacing, Expression for sensory presentation, Engine for demonstrated support needs, Stewardship for continuity.

The [Wing Index](../WING_INDEX.md) records allocation, review/completion and switching. One home wing per feather remains the rule; linked work does not count multiple times. [W1-F3 / historical Feather 57](../wings/W1-F3_THE_EIGHT_TIMELINES.md) remains parked.

This direction extends the [content-first phase closure](PHASE_CLOSURE_EPISTEMIC_FOUNDATIONS_2026-10-15.md). It changes development focus, not world canon or runtime truth.

## Depth, media and attention — 18 October 2026

Karun's direction favours approximately ten inhabited rooms over vast lifeless geography. This is a depth preference, not a fixed room cap or instruction to remove existing places. Every discernible feature shown in cinematic media should support closer attention through description, close-up imagery, interaction or contextual detail. Meaning need not mean a puzzle or reward. Contextual differences should follow authored conditions or recorded events.

Read the [W2 / W3 / W4 / W6 shared inspection checkpoint](../wings/W2-F3_POD_INSPECTION_AND_RECOGNITION.md). The first pod image remains a concept; generated detail is not silently canonised. Inspection vocabulary and mirror placement there are proposals, not a claim of working commands.
