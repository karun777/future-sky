# Water over the Rainbow Bridge

**Status:** Creative seed — deliberately unassigned; not established canon or a gameplay mechanic.  
**Origin:** Spontaneous phrase from Efiishent during discussion of the eight timelines and the protagonists' journeys across eras (16 October 2026).  
**Related:** Manzo Chapter One, eight timelines, cross-era relationships and remembrance.

> Water over the rainbow bridge.

## Resonances worth preserving

Not *water under the bridge*, as though the past were simply left behind, but **water over the rainbow bridge**: movement across a spectrum of possibility, worlds, eras and relationships.

A rainbow is not a fixed object; it appears through the relationship of light, water and an observer. A changed vantage changes the appearance. The bridge might connect not merely places but different ways of perceiving existence. The water keeps moving.

One provisional image: **the bridge may not need to be built; it may need to be perceived.**

## Stewardship

Preserve the original phrase verbatim. These interpretations are evocative suggestions, **not authorial decisions**. Do not assign the image to a particular timeline, character, event, power, cosmological mechanism or ending without further discussion. Return to it when the narrative earns its place.
