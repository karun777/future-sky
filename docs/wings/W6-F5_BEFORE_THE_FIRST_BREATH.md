## Five-step storyboard — candidate writing
### 1. Inclination

> Something draws your attention.
> You have followed it before.

Clear action: Choose a starting inclination.

Image direction: Luminous threads in deep space begin to organise into several distinct flowing paths; a quiet point of attention remains undefined. Suggest possibility, without dividing the image into fixed class symbols.

### 2. History

> Some journeys return as memories.
> Others still feel like dreams.

Clear action: Establish a little history, or leave it open.

Image direction: Layers of half-remembered inhabited spaces, distant music and star travel appear as delicate, partly transparent traces. Evoke a history whose dream/waking status is unresolved; do not depict identifiable people or literal documented events.

### 3. Embodiment

> Weight. Warmth.
> The possibility of touch.
> A shape you might call yours.

Clear action: Describe your embodiment; distinguish recognition from familiarity.

Image direction: Light begins acquiring material texture: woven cloth, warm metal, condensation and a suggestion of space around an absent occupant. No human silhouette, naked body, anatomy or predetermined physical traits. The subject is the possibility of inhabiting a body.

### 4. Gathering

> Enough to begin.
> Much remains beyond reach.

Clear action: Review, edit, or continue.

Image direction: Previously scattered luminous threads and translucent memory fragments gather around a quiet open centre. Integration without a soul diagram or literal metaphysical mechanism. Approaching coherence, with room left unresolved.

### 5. Awakening

> A sound reaches you.
> Then the surface beneath your fingers.

Clear action: Awaken, then follow your attention.

Image direction: Very close cinematic tactile fragments: woven berth cover in foreground, a softened warm-grey wall seam, faint blue light and a thin warm reflection. The first material foothold inside a functional habitable sci-fi pod. Do not show a full room layout, new props, a visible hand, a face or a clear exterior.
