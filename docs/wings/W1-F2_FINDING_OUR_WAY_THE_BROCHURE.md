## The scene already written

The character wakes without remembering boarding or arriving. Outside are rhythmic impacts and fragments of an argument: the Lounge was not supposed to open this cycle; someone wants the arrivals settled.

The brochure calls Triton Central the safest biodome in the Neptune Sector for six consecutive cycles. Inspecting it produces reassurance and the instruction, “Please disregard any contradictory onboarding information.” A loud metallic crash follows. The brochure does not acknowledge it.

These are authored perceptions, speech and promotional claims. They do not establish that the biodome is unsafe, the crash dangerous or the brochure deliberately deceptive.

## What it offers our chosen direction

| Layer | Existing evidence | Reading for review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplied orientation | Welcome, safety claim and instructions about contradictory information. | The arrival system offers a ready-made account of where the character is and how to respond. |
| Direct attention | Noise, voices, rhythm, interrupted conversation and the crash. | The character can attend to what the account does not explain. |
| Discernment | The separate “Question the situation” option examines the surroundings and asks whether this is a mistake. | Orientation can begin by noticing a gap between an explanation and experience, while withholding a verdict. |
| Agency | Listen, inspect, wait, investigate or question are authored options. | The character has several ways to seek bearings; questioning is an available choice, not the mandatory correct answer. |

**Working finding:** this scene already supports finding one's way when supplied guidance is incomplete or in tension with experience. We do not need to manufacture a guidance-system outage to explore that.

The concrete pairing is **automated onboarding information** and **direct attention plus judgement under uncertainty**. The brochure unfolds automatically; the source does not specify its internal technology or institutional owner.

## What this does not yet establish

The newcomer is disoriented; no ordinary resident is shown unable to cope. The scene therefore does not establish population-wide atrophy, a lost sensory power or technological dependence by itself.

The supplied account may be truthful, stale, selective, bureaucratic, comic or deceptive. The crash may be harmless maintenance. Direct perception is evidence to investigate, not an infallible alternative to technology.

The brochure and questioning passages are separate selectable options. Their existence does not prove that a player encounters both, in this order, or can select every option in one playthrough. No live playthrough or trigger/option-lifecycle audit was performed here.

In the authored option data, inspecting the brochure sets pod_brochure_read; questioning sets questioned_the_script. Neither option declares an ability unlock. Flag declarations are not proof of live delivery or persistence.

The archived arrival template provides earlier lineage, not a reason to overwrite the newer authored pod sequence. A character's limited knowledge is not author-level world truth.

## Authoring implication for review

Keep the tension and the humour. Let the character notice, listen and question without making either the brochure or the character automatically right.

This supplies a grounded first example of **orientation through discernment**. Whether Manzo residents routinely delegate that discernment, and what recovering it entails, remains an open world-state question.

No scene rewrite, mandatory lesson, new faction or mechanic is proposed by this analysis.
