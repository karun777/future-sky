# W2-F3 addendum — Preparation, Work Garments and Roles

**Source:** Karun, 18 October 2026, 12:40 Perth; preserved alongside [W4-F3](W4-F3_TWO_DAYS_AT_NEPTUNE_LOUNGE.md).  
**Home:** [W2-F3](W2-F3_THE_OPENING_WE_CAN_INHABIT.md), still in REVIEW.  
**Status:** Authorial direction recorded; exact garment designs and implementation pending.

## Pod as preparation space

Karun wants immediate immersion and close investigation: each wall, the back of a statue, both sides of a picked-up stone. Depth is preferred to uninhabited scale. The pod has an escape-room-like quality without absurd puzzle obstruction, and also serves as a construct/preparation space akin to the Matrix reference.

The character is an **existential spy**, awakening to fit themselves out. The enormous scope of possibilities should not be front-loaded; successive resets can reveal more of it. Do not turn that framing into an automatic spy faction membership, skill grant or exposition dump.

Basic character creation should normally be sorted **before leaving the pod**. Karun also entertains walking out immediately and being conspicuously unprepared or unclothed in the Lounge. These are not yet one settled exit rule: preserve the tension between expected preparation and possible early departure. No locked hatch or public reaction has been implemented by this direction.

## Explicit wardrobe scope

Work garments and casual gear should be separately browsable. Required work options:

| Work garment | Authored specification so far |
| --- | --- |
| Pilot uniform | Option required; pieces, insignia, pockets and access effects unspecified. |
| Military uniform | Option required; no faction or military affiliation assigned yet. |
| Cleaner's uniform | Option required; cleaners exist across factions. |
| Wizard uniform | Option required; capabilities and recognition unspecified. |
| Business attire | Option required; no employer or status automatically granted. |

Casual gear is explicitly required as a category; its actual list is not yet supplied. Do not substitute the earlier four implemented uniforms for these five options or delete existing choices without a migration decision.

Details should support inspection and meaningful outfitting. Exact contents and social consequences remain authoring work. Clothes, job, faction, Character identity and competence are distinct; the last distinction is a modelling safeguard rather than a new rule that a particular disguise succeeds or fails.

## Jobs and factions

Everyone needs a job. Some jobs may be dominated by particular factions, but jobs and factions are not interchangeable. Cleaners, and spies, can be found across factions.

Connect this to [W3's Character / Role / Driver distinctions](W3-F2_WHO_CARRIES_WHOM.md). A job can supply a situated role; a faction is not automatically the same thing as a job, class or current controller.
