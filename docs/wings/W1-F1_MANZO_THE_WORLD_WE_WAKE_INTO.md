## 1. Established foundation

The [Red Book v7.2](https://github.com/karun777/future-sky/blob/f44cf3eb0dbcbbef254881e9de76f836ed1c5fea/docs/RedBook_V7.2.md) establishes:

- Triton Central is a biodome town on Neptune's moon Triton, built atop alien ruins; artists, smugglers, dreamers and scholars live there.
- Manzo is expansive, strange and semi-stable. Technical sophistication has not removed terror, uncertainty or the work of becoming.
- The Neptune Lounge is a sanctuary for timeline refugees, a meeting place for resistance networks and a site of meta-identity rituals. Food, music, art and dubious exchanges make it a lived social place.
- Time travel is familiar enough to be a tool. The Cube is publicly displayed in the Lounge, routinely borrowed and returned to its borrowed moment; familiarity does not mean mastery.
- Characters encounter other selves and fractured memories. Efiishent and the Dark Rat Queen participate in these mysteries without being omniscient.
- The named factions exist as authored identities: Anamnesis Circle, Glitchbound, Woven Archive, Spiral Engineers and Divergents. Their names do not establish who governs Triton Central or controls its essentials.

These are world-lore statements, not assertions that every described capability is implemented.

## 2. Authorial direction carried by later working material

The [macro constellation research](https://github.com/karun777/future-sky/blob/dfe9e4a803bc75c852697af298c6d3f29c346c65/docs/research/THREE_ERAS_COMET_MACRO_CONSTELLATION_v0.2.md) records these authored directions. Its document status remains working research, not formally promoted canon:

| Direction | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| The comet's return is long anticipated and celebrated across the Earth system. | Celebratory texture does not settle everyone's beliefs or knowledge of danger. |
| The protagonists arrive separated, unsure how they got there and without memory of who they are. | This is their situation, not universal amnesia across Manzo. |
| They must find one another and consciously recover faculties used unconsciously during the earlier intervention. | Which faculties, and how they are recovered, remain open. |
| Technological mediation has allowed many inhabitants' direct faculties to atrophy. | Do not infer that all technology is harmful or all inhabitants are helpless. |
| Vitriol's Earth-directed threat is overcome; the comet becomes Earth's additional moon. | The outcome is an author-level given in the working outline; capture mechanics and participants' discoveries remain unresolved. |

[The Eternal Struggle](https://github.com/karun777/future-sky/blob/f44cf3eb0dbcbbef254881e9de76f836ed1c5fea/docs/current/THE_ETERNAL_STRUGGLE.md) adds a proposed narrative ethic: abundance does not finish the work of justice; leadership should enlarge others' agency; a cosmic dance of light and dark does not excuse harm. Its proposed-status header remains visible despite its presence on main.

Working synthesis for review: **Manzo has made extraordinary capabilities familiar. Our displaced protagonists must discover what those capabilities depend on, what people no longer exercise for themselves, and how to act together.**

That sentence is a reading of the sources, not newly established canon.

## 3. What we must leave open

- The government, infrastructure ownership, distribution of prosperity and balance of faction power. A Civic Authority or commercial syndicates are not established merely because they were proposed.
- Which faculties have atrophied, who retains them, and whether access to technological substitutes is equal.
- What an ordinary resident believes about the comet versus what Vitriol, his agents and the protagonists know. The cosmic revelation is not public knowledge.
- Precise chronology: the research places Manzo around 102,019 CE, approximately 100,000 years after Omincron. Do not silently replace earlier dates or collapse the Red Book's broader timeline language into the research topology.
- The relationship between seven Omincron lines and the proposed fixed Manzo history.
- Specific protagonist identities. Sera and the barmaid in [A Glass Trembling](https://github.com/karun777/future-sky/blob/dfe9e4a803bc75c852697af298c6d3f29c346c65/docs/research/narrative-context-trials/MANZO_TREMBLING_GLASS_TRIAL_v0.1.md) are exploratory candidate material, not confirmed Chapter One casting.
- Which authored experiences actually run. The [living-world inventory](https://github.com/karun777/future-sky/blob/f44cf3eb0dbcbbef254881e9de76f836ed1c5fea/docs/current/LIVING_WORLD_INVENTORY_v0.1.md) is a dated inventory, not a live walkthrough.

## 4. First world-state question

**What is one ordinary thing a Triton Central resident relies on technology to do, which our protagonists must learn to do directly?**

Suggested starting lens, for discussion only: finding one's way — through physical space, memory or relationships. The resident can obtain directions; the newcomer may have to discover orientation.

Choosing one example will let us ground dependence, retained capacity and everyday life in something a player could encounter at the Lounge. No answer is selected here, and this does not assign navigation powers to the compass.
