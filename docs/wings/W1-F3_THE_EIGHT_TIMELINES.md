## The seam to return to

**“Eight timelines” is a working proposition to reconcile, not a recovered fact.**

The sources contain several different uses of the same word:

- three eras sometimes called timelines in the Red Book;
- seven lines spanning three eras in the July Lore;
- seven Omincron lines A–G bounded by two fixed era histories in October research;
- possible histories, future attractors, lived histories, memories and perspectives that need not be identical categories.

Counting the research's fixed histories and A–G as separate entries would yield nine labels, but that arithmetic does not determine the world's topology. Neither “nine” nor “seven plus a new H” is adopted here. Eight could indicate a deliberate new authorial direction; its meaning waits for Karun.

The previous chat question—successive eras, branching realities, or both—was too broad to stand alone. There is already substantial three-era/A–G source material to build from.

The **eight development wings** organise our work. They do not establish a one-to-one relationship with in-world timelines. No such relationship is adopted.

**Completion check:** Karun reviews a coherent outline that explains its count and scope, preserves source lineage, distinguishes adopted decisions from possibilities, and leaves unresolved relationships explicit. A catalogue invented merely to fill eight slots does not satisfy the feather.
