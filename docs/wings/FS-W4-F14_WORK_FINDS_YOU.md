**W4 Play · Chapter 2 scenario proposal · 17 November 2026**

## The morning after

Efiishent wakes on the morning after Chapter 1. Give him a moment awake. Then a work notification comes through on his HUD.

In [Two Days at Neptune Lounge](W4-F3_TWO_DAYS_AT_NEPTUNE_LOUNGE.md), he and the princess fell asleep together after exchanging gifts. He has her gift and means to take it back to the pod to work out what to do with it. Where the notice catches him on that morning is open.

## First player beat

Proposed scope for this feather runs from waking to the first thing Efiishent can choose to do when the notice arrives. What can he tell from the notification itself? What would he have to do to learn more?
