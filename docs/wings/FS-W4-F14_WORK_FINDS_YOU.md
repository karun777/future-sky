# FS-W4-F14 — Work Finds You

**W4 Play · Chapter 2 scenario proposal · 17 November 2026**

## The morning after

Efiishent wakes on the morning after Chapter 1. Give him a moment awake. Then a work notification comes through on his HUD.

In [Two Days at Neptune Lounge](W4-F3_TWO_DAYS_AT_NEPTUNE_LOUNGE.md), he and the princess fell asleep together after exchanging gifts. He has her gift and means to take it back to the pod to work out what to do with it. Where the notice catches him on that morning is open.

## First player beat · draft for review

Shortly after he wakes, three lines settle into focus behind his eyes.

> DRAGONFLY RESCUE · CREW CALL
>
> Routine comet catch out of Triton Central.
>
> Drinks and briefing at Neptune Lounge.

Efiishent can inspect the crew call or leave it waiting while he attends to the gift. Opening it could show the crew sheet. Whether his name already occupies a line is an authoring question. The crew call wording and interaction are proposed. The sender, his berth aboard and any link to the gift are open.

## Notebook page 1 · Dragonfly Rescue

“Catch a Comet.” The handwritten mission reference appears to read “NLW7F777”; retain it as a notebook reference pending confirmation. “How many monkeys do we need?” Six roles follow in order: pilot, kitchen, engineering, portal engineering/navigation, medic and DJ. Underneath them Karun wrote **TEAM RESONANCE**. What the crew can accomplish together is a thread to develop. How resonance works is open.

Two failure possibilities appear on the page. The comet kills a space whale on Neptune, or the payload is lost to a rival crew.

## Notebook page 2 · An Undertaking

The sketch places Dragonfly Rescue off Triton, with Neptune, a space whale, a portal net, a comet and a second crew in the same field. The numbered account gives the wider chapter its sequence.

1. Dragonfly Rescue sets out from Triton Central space dock on a routine comet-catching gig.
2. The crew gathers for drinks and a briefing at Neptune Lounge. The order of muster and physical departure can be settled in staging.
3. Telemetry confirms the pickup and identifies a comet payload. The quoted word looks like “Omincron”; its spelling and meaning here need Karun’s reading.
4. The portal net catches the comet. The crew secures and analyses the payload.
5. They send the payload in the “mail” to Jupiter.
6. A second crew intercepts the payload. The handwritten crew label appears to read “slayer crew”.
7. A space chase follows to recover it. Recovery remains an outcome to play for.

The interception starts the chase even after a successful catch. Losing the payload to the rival crew is a possible ending. These later beats are author-facing until Efiishent encounters them in play.
