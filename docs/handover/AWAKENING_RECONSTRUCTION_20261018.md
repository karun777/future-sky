## The opening, understood as a whole

Efiishent wakes in a body he does not recognise.

The room is small enough to take seriously. Its walls, storage, lights, garment, sounds and view are available to attention. There is a whole civilisation outside, and he is late to understanding his place in it.

He does not need to begin with a burning desire to recover his biography. There are more immediate questions: whose hands are these; where is a mirror; what is he wearing; what is that signal; can he leave; what is expected of him?

Then an ordinary obligation gives the opening its momentum.

**He has a gig tomorrow night.**

He cannot yet say what kind of set, what instrument, what preparation, which collaborators, who precedes him or who has come to listen. Somewhere in those practical uncertainties is an appointment after the music. Preparing to enter a living social world becomes the way into the larger mission.

That is what binds today's material together: **embodiment, preparation, participation, recognition and a gift whose significance reaches beyond the game.** The world remains real within the story even when Efiishent cannot be sure what he has experienced. It leaves traces for other people, with other memories and other reasons to be there.

This paragraph is a synthesis of Karun's account, not additional canon about a missing institution or mechanism.

## 1. What kind of awakening is this?

### Adopted canon

Towards the end of his ordinary earthly human life during the Omincron period, Efiishent designed and manufactured a cube in which to preserve his brain. He left instructions for revival once a colony had been established on Triton. His awakening is part of the imaginative future-scenario exploration Karun is undertaking personally.

In Manzo, naturally born and laboratory-manufactured humans are individual souls continuing the unfolding of their samskaras. Ordinarily they do not have access to memories of past lives.

The preserved-brain minority is different. Old brainers retain memories, skills and faculties, but retention does not mean immediate access. Rebirth is less traumatic than natural birth, yet reintegration is still a process.

**Usually seven days. Only if players read the instruction manual. There is no known instruction manual.**

Keep all three statements together. The manual is neither an available tutorial item nor a quest requirement we have already designed.

| Population category | Share |
| --- | ---: |
| Natural birth | 10% |
| Manufactured birth | 89% |
| Preserved-brain rebirth — old brainers | 1% |

Old brainers remain a novelty and a curiosity. In-world Manzo calls these reborn-brain humans “players”; the project also uses player for the human participating through an interface. Both meanings need to remain legible.

A body akin to the previous one supports continuity. A more foreign chosen body demands greater effort toward parallelism. The particular body-selection process and degree of difficulty remain to be authored.

See the [canon note](../current/MANZO_PRESERVED_BRAINS_AND_AWAKENING.md) for the authoritative decision record.

### What this changes in the opening

The unfamiliar hands are no longer merely an atmospheric mystery. They are the first embodied encounter with a significant part of the world's premise.

The mirror, clothing and movement can participate in reintegration without becoming a lecture about it. Someone may discover a skill before recovering the memory that explains it; that is a **possible scene direction**, not a claim that we have settled the order in which memory and capability return.

A gig within a two-day window can place demands on a person still reintegrating. We have not fixed the window's exact alignment with the seven-day process. There is no reason to wait for an invented “memory complete” milestone before the story begins.

### The cubes remain a question worth preserving

The brain-preservation cube, the CMYK Cube's cross-era history, the wooden cube at the neck, the pod's older cubic form and the implemented personal Cube storage all exist as references in the material.

They may eventually connect. Their shape does not establish that they are one physical object, unrelated objects or a solved recursion. Record the relationships when Karun supplies them.

## 2. The pod — a place to inhabit and prepare

The older arrival describes a square, habitable cubicle: spartan, functional, comfortable, wizardy and high-tech. The new cinematic study supplies intimacy, material texture and a room-facing view, but its rounded weathered geometry is a visual proposal.

The pod has the quality of an escape room and a construct/preparation space. It teaches interaction through useful attention: examine, listen, turn, open, dress, read, look again. It should not need absurd locks or a long command manual to justify staying.

The scope can grow through resets. A first arrival need not expose every compartment, possibility or way of inhabiting the world. A returning participant should gain depth through accumulated familiarity, not face a fresh mandatory tutorial.

### A first passage — candidate writing

Cold blue light.

You lift your hands.

They move when you mean them to.

You turn them over. Open them. Close them.

You do not recognise them.

For a moment you forget to breathe.

Then a breath comes. Something cold rests against your chest. A thin chain disappears beneath your garment.

Beyond the wall:

**THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.**

Machinery. Probably.

You listen.

“…not ready…”

“…the Lounge wasn’t supposed to open this cycle…”

“…just get them settled…”

You sit up.

Condensation beads across a viewport. Beside the hatch, a red light burns steadily. Below it is a button.

A brochure protrudes from a dispenser.

**WELCOME TO TRITON CENTRAL.**  
**Safest Biodome in the Neptune Sector.**  
**Six consecutive cycles.**

There is a world outside. It sounds occupied.

---

This is review prose, drawing on the existing arrival and current Thread Node. It is not installed content. The “you listen” sentence is attention within the opening, not a claim that the player has selected the existing Listen option or earned its flag. A final implementation should avoid narrating a substantial player decision before they make it.

### Hands, mirror and FEED7

The older authored sequence notices unfamiliar hands early, then breathing, chest sensation, hum/scan, HUD and a wider view after sitting up. FEED7 is encountered as attention turns to a display in the cubicle.

That is a useful **viewpoint order**, not a compulsory itinerary. A player who immediately searches for their face should receive a relevant response.

The mirror inside the wardrobe door is a concrete proposal, not recovered canon. It uses an existing local compartment, while a weak reflection in the wet viewport can acknowledge the immediate attempt. If we expose a mirror, we must eventually show or describe a face drawn from that Character's agreed appearance. An endlessly obscured mirror would betray the promise.

FEED7 belongs early in the available room, but not necessarily in the first crop. Existing source provides a nearby display and the command. The current command returns a website link; the older fullscreen/return presentation is intent, not shipped behaviour.

The initial cinematic image contains no visible hands. It can accompany surveying the room after the embodied first beat. It is not the only possible camera position within the pod.

### Character creation as preparation

Karun wants basic character creation sorted before ordinary departure. The latest possibility also permits a hurried, poorly prepared exit, with social consequences in the Lounge.

We have not chosen an exit policy. The current game does not implement a wardrobe-completion lock. Rather than prematurely treating those two intentions as a contradiction, design the normal invitation toward preparation and then deliberately author what a refusal does.

“Character creation” also needs to distinguish:

- choices supplied by the human participant;
- bodily and personal details the awakening Character can perceive;
- remembered identity that has not yet returned;
- job, clothes and a role someone is preparing to perform.

Existing creation asks for name, birthdate and class before the pod scene. The new embodied opening may require a different presentation, but the current system is not silently rewritten by this document.

The character is an **existential spy**. A uniform is one way of entering the world, not a complete definition of the being wearing it. Everyone needs a job; cleaners and spies may work within or across different factions.

## 3. Cinematic media makes a promise

Stills and occasional short videos carry substantial storytelling weight. They can give players a concrete foothold without exhausting what remains to imagine.

Every discernible thing in a scene supports closer attention. That might mean a text description, a close-up, another side, a physical response or recognition that depends on context.

The obligation is to the player's reasonable attention, not to simulating every pixel. Grouping screws and seams into a coherent panel is sensible; refusing to acknowledge a conspicuous book, button or plant is not.

Inspecting is distinct from operating. Looking at a button need not press it. Seeing a garment need not wear it. An object can have ordinary significance without becoming a puzzle or loot.

The [complete pod inspection pass](../wings/W2-F3_POD_INSPECTION_AND_RECOGNITION.md) accounts for the first image, including the plants, books, vessels, textiles and fittings image generation introduced. It remains proposed content. Retain those objects with support or revise the image; do not silently turn generated decoration into established lore.

Time, position, person, knowledge and actual world change can reveal different detail. The variants should agree with one another. A closer view might make a title readable; relevant experience might make it recognisable. Those are different discoveries.

The same principle will apply to a statue's back and a stone's other side. It gives a small world depth.

## 5. Efiishent's two-day passage

This is the first account to render, not a universal list of actions every player must copy.

He comes from Omincron through a portal he stepped through at Earthdance 2024 in the Omincron era. He is to meet a representative of the allied alien sadvipran embassy. In his particular encounter, the ambassador, the princess and the Dark Rat Queen are the same person. There is a gift to obtain, and Vitriol's agents are trying to intercept it.

He wakes without knowing much and without necessarily caring to remember. The gig tomorrow gives him a reason to orient. Fairly early he discovers there is someone to meet after his set.

What he does not know is that a poor performance means the ambassador does not stay for the following set. Preparing and playing matter because they alter a person's reason to remain in the place.

He builds the atmosphere for the next artist, hands over, makes the urgent trip to the toilet and returns. The transition has left the floor a little stagnant. The artist is playing beautifully. Efiishent helps bring the floor alive and enters flow.

The princess arrives. Attraction and musical attention become shared resonance. They are participating with others, while the particular intensity between them is conspicuous enough to move people and attract sinister attention.

They recognise one another with a certainty they cannot yet comfortably explain. They also notice why the dance floor is no place for the exchange. They find a room and believe they have been discreet.

They make love. Afterwards they speak of what they know. Efiishent gives her the Tiny Book; she gives him her gift. They sleep together.

In the morning he must return to the pod with an item and a practical problem: what is it, what now, and how does it get back to Omincron?

The princess is the Dark Rat Queen. That identity connection must not become general knowledge; discovery by Vitriol's agents would endanger her. Someone noticing the pair does not automatically know who she is or what passed between them.

For Karun, the gift's true relevance or meaning requires its receipt from her **IRL**. That is part of this particular relationship between lived experience and authored world. It should not be flattened into a generic inventory victory or a demand for automatic real-world verification.

The [primary account](AUTHORIAL_NOTES_20261018.md) preserves the author's wording and the source's unresolved “get from Omincron” phrase. The return direction is not silently repaired here.

## 6. One quest, many particular passages

**Any player can undertake the quest to meet a representative of an allied faction at Neptune Lounge and make a trade. A romantic connection is possible, not required.**

That is the common quest. Efiishent's performance, connection with the princess and private exchange are his particular passage through it. They do not make every participant a performer or make romance the price of completing diplomacy.

| Layer | What it describes |
| --- | --- |
| Common quest | Meet an allied-faction representative and make a trade within the available opportunity. |
| Particular instance | The people, appointments, motives, access conditions, offers and consequences of this encounter. |
| Participant perspective | What each person perceives, remembers, believes and can establish. |
| Persistent footprint | What this occurrence leaves in the world, with privacy governing access and disclosure. |

These are authoring distinctions using the existing Scenario, Character and Role vocabulary.

Karun used the Puzzler to illustrate another participant undertaking this same mission. The Puzzler's particular version, and the Dark Rat Queen's own particular version, will be authored later. Neither is just a copy of Efiishent's experience with a changed name. We have not assigned their counterpart, gift, romantic involvement or route.

Different participants can attempt the quest at different times or converge in the same period. They may be present at one event with different purposes and different knowledge. The event can be shared without making their outcomes or interpretations identical.

The recurring princes and princesses belong to the Lounge's social life. They may be representatives, partners, traders or people with purposes of their own as authored. A royal title alone does not establish diplomatic credentials, allegiance or willingness to exchange.

Efiishent's princess is specifically the sadvipran embassy representative and the Dark Rat Queen. His secret identity connection remains protected. That particular encounter is not automatically made available to every participant.

### Footprints with privacy paramount

Every quest leaves a footprint. That does **not** mean every completion is publicly announced, every identity can be inferred, or private encounters become a searchable public record.

World truth, evidence and permission to learn it are distinct. The occurrence can persist while its participants, private conversation, intimacy, gift contents and identifying details remain protected.

A footprint may be accessible only to its participants or reveal limited, non-identifying detail to others. Even the existence of an appointment or exchange can be sensitive; it is not automatically public. Specific discoverable traces should be authored with their access conditions and what they actually reveal.

Deep curiosity can uncover permitted traces. It does not grant a blanket right to another participant's private history. A later participant's version of the quest can be informed by the past without consuming someone else's intimacy as content.

**Authoring proposal:** for each trace, record its source occurrence, who may discover it, what it reveals, and what remains private. This is a small content obligation, not a new surveillance or public quest-feed system.

In a future version, another human may inhabit a role currently driven by script. The Character / Role / Driver distinctions already support thinking about this. A handover must preserve identity, knowledge, commitments and the difference between a human decision and scripted continuity. The live game does not yet implement general human/NPC role transfer.

## 7. Logic that serves the account

A dependency map helps us ask what makes the story possible. It should not confuse an account of one successful passage with a script the system forces every participant to perform.

### The strongest specified gate in Efiishent's instance

**If Efiishent plays poorly, the ambassador does not remain for the following set.**

This gate belongs to Efiishent's authored instance, not to every version of the diplomatic quest. Playing well keeps that possibility alive. It does not compel a meeting, attraction, recognition, privacy or an exchange. We have not designed a quality score.

### The time window

The two-day span gives arrival and preparation their pressure. “Tomorrow night,” “following set,” and “next morning” provide order before they provide exact timestamps.

The unresolved next-morning boundary is local: it can be addressed when scheduling this window. It is not a reason to stop writing the scene or redesign world time.

The exact clock scale, late entry, opportunity-closing times and reset retention are still open. The nightly visit and the possible two-day chapter repeat are different rhythms.

### Three logical outcomes are useful

| Answer | Meaning in authoring |
| --- | --- |
| Possible | The currently declared necessary conditions are met; someone still has to act. |
| Closed | A declared condition fails, or the specified opportunity has expired. |
| Unresolved | Necessary information or an authoring decision is missing. |

“Unresolved” is not failure and should not become success by default. These are rehearsal answers, not newly canonised runtime statuses.

### A repeat preserves history

We must distinguish renewed opportunities from erased occurrences. A new evening can bring another royal visitor while an earlier exchange still happened. A new cycle can reveal another layer of the pod while a gift already given remains in someone else's custody.

The exact reset policy is not fixed. Its eventual rules must respect the author's premise that the world remembers.

### Executable authoring rehearsal

A small [offline rehearsal](../../tools/authoring/chapter_one_rehearsal.py) and [23 boundary cases](../../tools/authoring/test_chapter_one_rehearsal.py) exercise these distinctions with synthetic ticks and fictional fixtures.

They test the common meeting/trade opportunity without a romance requirement, then Efiishent's good performance without guaranteed attendance; expired opportunities; privacy and willingness; one occurrence with different observations; facts surviving disputed credit; non-duplicating gift custody; and traces preserved after a sample cycle and reload.

The sample cycle-retention and half-open interval are **explicit rehearsal policies**, not decisions about the live chapter. Quality and willingness are supplied facts, not inferred scores. No production service imports this code.

The rehearsal shows that the logic can be made precise. It does not claim a playable quest, Discord delivery, concurrent transactions, security isolation, a finished reset policy or established paths for every participant.

## 8. Objects whose purpose we do not yet understand

The Red Book already gives uncertain-use artefacts and exchanges a place in this world. The actual feather-and-metal gift Karun received today belongs in the collected trail without being labelled irrelevant or forced into the Queen's mission.

It has observed form, reported giver/recipient, a date and unresolved purpose. A later connection may emerge. Until then the provenance is enough to preserve it.

The Tiny Book is a stronger recovered continuity lead than the earlier W4-F3 language conveyed: prior author notes explicitly describe a rat-sized gift for the Queen, retrieved from Efiishent's office/quarters. The current item definition, image and gifting callback corroborate an implemented strand. Its live position is not the original office placement; portable items can have moved.

That does not prove every later detail or item instance belongs to this particular encounter. It means the author has already laid a trail we should follow instead of treating the book as an empty placeholder.

## 9. What changed in this reconstruction

- The narrative comes first. Classification supports it.
- The opening is reconnected to preserved-brain awakening and reintegration.
- Hands, mirror-seeking, FEED7, clothing and the gig are understood as parts of one arrival.
- The Tiny Book is restored as a concrete earlier thread.
- The wardrobe remains connected to work, jobs and factions, rather than cosmetic menu expansion.
- Cinematic imagery is held accountable to investigation.
- Shared history and private knowledge are demonstrated in a bounded rehearsal.
- Earlier work is retained with its authority and review status. Research was not merged into canon by resemblance.
- Reconnection enabled live verification. It did not make GitHub sources suddenly more authoritative or absolve earlier loss of synthesis.
