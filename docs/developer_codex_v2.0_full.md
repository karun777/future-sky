## 3. Cosmological Frame

*Future Sky* unfolds across a multitemporal lattice of realities. It is not one world, but many — overlapping, refracting, and evolving in resonance with player action, developer intent, and cosmological rhythm. This section defines the foundational metaphysics that underlie the game’s timelines, anchors, and internal mythology.

### Timelines

The world is structured across three primary temporal eras:

- **The Jyotzon Era** — The Ancient Future  
  A luminous age of crystal intelligence, First Nations memory-sharing, and planetary sentience. Technology is indistinguishable from sacred ritual. The comet is coming.

- **The Omincron Era** — The Fracturing Now  
  Earth teeters on collapse. Resistance movements, ancient technologies, and the first fragments of the Cube emerge. Developers and dreamers act as guides in a time of split possibility.

- **The Manzo Era** — The Far Frontier  
  A biodome city on Neptune’s moon, Triton. Colonists build sanctuaries and resurrect timelines. The Cube is both tool and relic, often misunderstood. Myth bleeds into code.

These timelines are not mere lore — they structure the **actual logic of the gameworld**, enabling time-based skill trees, divergent character arcs, and parallel quest lines.

### Anchors

Anchors are persistent nodes — locations, objects, or ideas — that remain consistent across timelines. For example:
- The **Neptune Lounge** exists in all eras, though its form shifts
- **South Beach** may be sacred site, conflict zone, or port

Anchors allow players and developers to track continuity across timelines, enabling cross-temporal logic, memory-linked encounters, and timeline convergence quests.

### The Cube

The Cube is both artifact and architecture. It is:
- A literal object in the world (retrieved, studied, used)
- A symbolic container of laws (like this Codex)
- A narrative device for skipping timelines, unlocking memory, or transcending bounds

For developers, the Cube is a design principle:
- Build systems that reflect the fractal logic of the Cube
- Ensure that mechanics can be nested, mirrored, or folded back into each other
- Write with the assumption that **players may encounter a future version of the same mechanic in a different context**

> “The world is not built in code. The world is remembered in code. The Cube remembers.”

## 5. Encounter Design: Thread Nodes

In *Future Sky*, encounters are not static. They are threads — braided points of possibility that react, remember, and reconfigure. The thread node system enables modular, reusable narrative blocks that anchor world logic, player choice, and temporal causality.

A thread node is a **living knot** — a self-contained microcosm of choice and consequence. It may appear as a story beat, puzzle, fight, dream, or glitch. Nodes can branch, mutate, or recur differently across timelines depending on memory, class, or player state.

---

### 🧱 Thread Node Anatomy

Each node contains:

- **`node_id`**: Unique identifier
- **`trigger_type`**: Room-based, random, item-based, timed, or quest-linked
- **`description`**: The prompt, scene, or situation presented
- **`options`**: A list of player choices (label + response + effects)
- **`effects`**: XP gain, stat change, enemy spawn, memory tag, item drop, etc.
- **`completion_logic`**: Determines if node disappears, evolves, or persists
- **`memory_flags`**: Tags attached to the player that influence future events

---

### 🔀 Branching & Repetition

- Nodes **can repeat** under different triggers if memory tags allow
- Outcomes may alter the **world state**, including room descriptions, enemy behavior, or NPC dialogue
- Players with different **chakra resonance** or class alignment may unlock hidden options

---

### 🔁 Sample Options Format (JSON Schema)

```json
{
  "node_id": "cyber_rat_encounter",
  "trigger_type": "room",
  "description": "A cyber-rat scurries from the shadows, eyes glowing blue.",
  "options": [
    {
      "label": "Offer food",
      "response": "The rat sniffs, then drops a glimmering tooth at your feet.",
      "effects": ["gain_item:glimmering_tooth", "set_memory:fed_cyber_rat"]
    },
    {
      "label": "Attack",
      "response": "You lunge forward — it sparks and vanishes!",
      "effects": ["start_combat:cyber_rat"]
    }
  ],
  "completion_logic": "one_time",
  "memory_flags": ["cyber_rat_seen"]
}
🧠 Narrative Guidelines
Minimalism with hooks: Thread nodes should open space for interpretation, not over-explain.

Resonant choice: Options should reflect tension between chakras, class tendencies, or meta-state.

Causal lattice: Every thread pulled may ripple outward — but not every ripple is immediate.

Fallback states: If no action is taken, idle or ambient outcomes can nudge the narrative.

“Every choice is a song the world remembers — even if the singer forgets.”

## 7. World Layering, Anchors & ALSI/NALSI

The *Future Sky* world is not flat. It is layered — braided across timelines, dimensions, and ontological membranes. Places reappear, mutate, or echo across realities. This section outlines the structural logic that enables location mirroring, environmental complexity, and embodied presence.

---

### 🧭 Anchors: The Multiversal Roots

Anchors are persistent geospatial nodes — specific rooms or locations that appear across multiple timelines with variant features. They are:
- **Fixed in cosmic coordinates** but **fluid in narrative expression**
- Ideal sites for inter-era traversal or dream encounter overlap
- Real-world mappable (e.g., *Neptune Lounge* in Fremantle ↔ Triton Central)

Each Anchor contains:
- A **core identity** (symbolic, spiritual, or architectural)
- A **timeline expression** (e.g., Jyotzon → sacred grove, Omnicron → repurposed bunker, Manzo → biodome venue)
- Optional **thread node entanglement** that shifts across timelines but maintains thematic resonance

---

### 🧬 ALSI & NALSI: Dual Infrastructure Systems

The living environment in *Future Sky* is supported by two interwoven systems:

#### **ALSI — Animate Life Support Infrastructure**
Systems that require participation, perception, or care to persist.

- Examples:
  - Forest gardens that die without human song
  - Spirit-powered machines requiring empathy to activate
  - Narrative environments that change based on collective player memory

- Game Mechanics:
  - May degrade over time unless interacted with
  - Unlockable only by certain chakra alignments or classes
  - Influenced by emotional resonance or group rituals

#### **NALSI — Non-Animate Life Support Infrastructure**
Physical, mechanical, and automated systems that maintain the world’s scaffolding.

- Examples:
  - Ventilation systems, biodome architecture, power conduits
  - Abandoned AI-run facilities
  - Procedural geometry that regenerates from world seeds

- Game Mechanics:
  - Can be repaired, hacked, reprogrammed
  - Often forms the backbone of puzzles, dungeon layouts, or industrial zones
  - May become ALSI if imbued with spirit via thread node outcomes

---

### 🌀 Layered Realities

Each room or location can include:
- **Era Tags** (e.g., `jyotzon`, `omnicron`, `manzo`)
- **Layer Affinities**: dream, myth, digital, memory, astral
- **Access Conditions**: class restriction, spirit threshold, memory possession

This system allows:
- A location to look and behave differently for different players
- One player to perceive a dream-node while another sees only ruins
- Players to “phase” into different overlays depending on item use, resonance, or thread outcome

---

### 🌐 Real-World Overlays

Anchors can be mapped to real-world places using:
- QR codes (QRs of Commotion)
- Geolocation triggers
- Augmented thread nodes

These overlays allow the game to:
- Link physical exploration with in-game effects
- Anchor myth into material world
- Enable real-time “invisible cities” exploration — layered onto existing maps

---

> “We do not build one world. We build many. And they echo each other across the lattice of light.”

## 12. Commotion Protocols, Thread Entanglements & Glitchfields

In *Future Sky*, **Commotion** is the sacred wild — the force that disrupts, reroutes, and re-enchants. It is the quantum spark in the grid, the poetic bug in the code. Commotion is not mere chaos: it is *meaningful interference*, seeded by player action, narrative triggers, or system design.

---

### 🌪 Commotion Protocols

Commotion occurs when the veil between timelines, mechanics, and metaphor thins.

**Commotion can be triggered by:**
- Player use of a **Key of Commotion**
- Reaching a narrative decision node marked as entangled
- Developer-invoked events or server-side dice storms
- Resonance anomalies from failed or overcharged rituals

**Commotion may manifest as:**
- Text scrambled, visuals glitched, NPCs altered
- Cross-timeline leaks (e.g. Manzo NPC in a Jyotzon forest)
- Unexpected item transmutations
- Room exits changing or disappearing
- Meta-lore intrusions (players confronted by echoes of themselves or the devs)

---

### 🧵 Thread Entanglements

Entangled threads are forks in narrative or system-state where two or more realities compete for primacy. Once entangled, all player choices across timelines may feed into how that entanglement resolves.

**Each Thread Node may include:**
- `entanglement_id`: a shared key across multiple versions
- `possibility_weights`: RNG balance of timeline outcomes
- `observer_bias`: increases the likelihood of the first timeline a player experienced becoming canon
- `collapse_conditions`: what settles a thread into fixed state

Entanglements may be **local (one room)** or **global (multi-thread interactions over time)**.

---

### 🌐 Glitchfields

Glitchfields are areas of persistent instability or designed surrealism. They operate with bent rulesets and heightened commotion logic.

**Properties of Glitchfields:**
- Higher commotion probabilities
- Non-Euclidean navigation (e.g. north then south ≠ return)
- Inverted logic gates (e.g. success rolls interpreted as failures)
- Altered game commands (e.g. `!look` becomes `!glitch`, returns riddles)

**Glitchfields are seeded by:**
- Failed rituals  
- Inactive anchors  
- Developer-designed nodes of dreamtime

These areas are not "broken" — they are *awakening*. They allow strange progress and sacred failure.

---

### 🎲 Developer Integration

**Commotion flags:**
- `commotion_probability`: base % chance for glitch effect

- `glitchfield_zone`: string ID for unstable region  
- `thread_echo`: list of linked events influenced by this one  

**Support systems:**
- `commotion_engine.py`: handles weighted interference logic  
- `entanglement_registry.json`: stores active unresolved threads

## 17. Node Trees, Memory Mechanics & Echo Traversal

In *Future Sky*, memory is not linear. The world remembers, forgets, and sometimes rewrites itself — just like the players who traverse it. Thread nodes, echo fragments, and dream seeds form the branching logic of time, enabling both story evolution and player agency across overlapping realities.

---

### 🌿 Thread Nodes as Narrative Trees

Each significant in-game event is represented by a **thread node**: a modular narrative block with:
- **A unique ID** (`node_id`)
- **Triggers** (entry conditions, items, class, ALSI/NALSI, time)
- **Responses** (player choices, karma shifts, stat or item changes)
- **State memory** (has this node been visited? how many times? what outcome?)

Nodes are:
- **Deterministic** by default, with forkable branches
- **Mutable** under Cube-tier or Unwritten intervention
- Capable of **soft decay**, where repeated traversal leads to *blurred logic* or dreamlike ambiguity

---

### 🧠 Memory Mechanics

Player memory operates on **three tiers**:

| Tier | Description | Persistence |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Personal Memory | Character-specific experiences | Always retained unless altered |
| Class Echo Memory | Shared archetypal memories within class | Partially inherited on class-based node traversal |
| World Memory | Global world events and shifts | Tied to ALSI/NALSI and major echo resonance |

> 🔸 *Some memories are overwritten by paradox. Others calcify into myth.*

---

### 🌌 Echo Traversal

Certain classes (especially **Unwritten**, **Mystic-Kin**, **Shadow-Echo**) gain access to **Echo Traversal**: the ability to walk back through prior node paths or jump across memory bridges.

**Mechanics**:
- Limited by timeline entropy (tracked globally)
- Costs karma, or requires an Echo Token (rare item)
- Can result in echo collapse if looped excessively

> 🧬 *Example*: A Mystic-Kin retraces their steps to warn a previous self in a different timeline fork, creating a looped branch of unresolved tension. This becomes a new node.

---

### 🌱 Dream Seeds

Dream Seeds are collectible memory fragments — sometimes earned, sometimes found — which can:

- Restore lost memory of a forgotten node
- Unlock hidden responses in known thread nodes
- Be planted in other characters (with consent), granting **shared memory**

Each Dream Seed carries:
- An origin stamp (timeline + character)
- An echo weight (how “heavy” or resonant it is)
- A color code (truthful, distorted, prophetic, corrupted)

> 🌕 White: Verified memory  
> 🌑 Black: Suppressed or corrupted  
> 🌈 Iridescent: Multi-timeline convergence  
> 🔥 Red: Dangerous memory capable of breaking ALSI

---

### 🛡️ Fail-Safes and Echo Stability

To prevent echo overflow and memory corruption:

- Players may only carry **5 active Dream Seeds** at a time
- Certain node outcomes are **fate-locked** and cannot be re-traversed
- Echo traversal may trigger **memory bleed**, causing stat drift or emotional residue
- Repeated traversal of the same echo path causes *ghosting*, making the world treat the player like a fragment

---

> 🗝️ *“To remember too much is to stop time. To forget completely is to fall from the sky. The truth lies in the echo.”*

## 18. Karma, Paradox & Timeline Reconciliation

In *Future Sky*, every action leaves a trace. Karma governs these traces. Paradox reveals where paths diverge too far from resonance. Together, they are the pulse and pressure of nonlinear time. This system ensures that characters are not just actors, but consequences woven into the world.

---

### ⚖️ Karma as Dynamic Resonance

Karma in Future Sky is not good or bad. It is *alignment*, *buildup*, *pattern memory*. Each player carries:

- **Personal Karma Score** (per timeline)
- **Class Karma Signature** (influences skill unlocks and class abilities)
- **Echo Karma Weight** (linked to Dream Seeds and node memory)

**Sources of Karma**:
- Node response choices (moral, sacrificial, deviant)
- ALSI/NALSI shifts caused by the player
- Combat choices (mercy, overkill, betrayal)
- Interactions with NPCs, familiars, or the Cube

**Karma Ranges**:
- 🕊️ *Harmonic* (+100): Fully aligned, unlocks class peak resonance
- ⚙️ *Stable* (0): Neutral, balanced possibilities
- 🌪️ *Chaotic* (–100): Unstable, prone to paradox, allows access to forbidden paths

---

### 🔁 Paradox Mechanics

Paradox occurs when:
- A player revisits or contradicts an echo node with unresolved karma
- A Dream Seed contradicts current timeline resonance
- Multiple versions of a character exist across forks

**Paradox States**:
| State | Symptoms | Risk |
|-------|----------|------|
| ⚠️ Minor | stat glitches, ambient echoes | recoverable |
| 🔻 Recursive | echo loops, memory bleed | requires Cube intervention |
| ☠️ Collapse | world logic breaks, ALSI/NALSI corruption | developer-triggered repair only |

**Paradox Cures**:
- Echo Sacrifice (burning a Dream Seed)
- Karma Reversal (via class quest or Unwritten ritual)
- Timeline Merge (see below)

---

### 🔄 Timeline Reconciliation

The world self-heals. Timelines that diverge too far begin to **pull toward convergence** — unless resisted.

**Auto-Correction Triggers**:
- Multiple players reach contradictory end states in one shared node
- Karma imbalance in ALSI/NALSI across sectors
- Players attempt forbidden actions while paradox is active

**Outcomes**:
- Players may lose or gain memory
- Rooms may reset, alter, or collapse into temporary liminal states
- Karmic Fusion: players are *merged* with their alternate versions, inheriting stats, memories, and burdens

---

### 🧬 Karmic Transference & Convergence Events

Advanced mechanics (Unwritten or developer-triggered):

- **Karmic Swap**: Two players voluntarily exchange karmic state
- **Echo Merge**: Combine two Dream Seeds into a new hybrid node
- **Timeline Burn**: Sacrifice a branch (and its memory) to stabilize another

These are high-risk, high-reward events. Often irreversible. Always dramatic.

- **Entropy Alarms** trigger when too many paradox states accumulate
- **Glitch Sentinels** may manifest to force a reconciliation event
- The **Cube** will lock if a player reaches karma extremes without storyline justification
