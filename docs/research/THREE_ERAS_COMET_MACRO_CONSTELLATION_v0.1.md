## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the first boundary of the larger Future Sky narrative field.

The macro structure contains:

- three eras;
- three era-scale Anchor constellations;
- three comets, one associated with each era;
- Efiishent and Lord Vitriol present within all three contexts;
- seven timelines to be considered later across the complete three-era structure.

This document does not yet define the seven timelines or fully populate any era constellation.

The first detailed constellation will be Manzo.

## 3. Current macro Anchors

The following propositions are treated as working givens for this research field.

### A-001 — Three eras

The macro narrative spans:

1. Jyotzon;
2. Ominicron;
3. Manzo.

### A-002 — Three era constellations

Each era forms one major Anchor constellation.

An era constellation may contain:

- one comet;
- places and Institutions;
- Characters and versions of Characters;
- historical givens;
- epistemic boundaries;
- civilizational pressures;
- continuity Anchors;
- variable outcomes;
- unresolved questions.

### A-003 — Three comets

There are three comets within the macro field: one associated with each era.

This document does not yet assert whether the comets:

- are materially unrelated;
- belong to one larger cycle;
- carry related life or information;
- are expressions of one deeper phenomenon;
- communicate across eras;
- or have been mistaken for one another.

The count of three is presently anchored. Their deeper relationship remains open.

### A-004 — Cross-context Efiishent

Efiishent is present within all three era contexts.

This may involve different versions, incarnations, expressions or historically connected selves. Exact identity and continuity across the three eras remain to be mapped.

Presence does not imply omniscience.

### A-005 — Cross-context Vitriol

Lord Vitriol is present within all three era contexts.

The form of that presence may differ by era. This document does not yet decide whether every appearance is:

- one continuous consciousness;
- a version or incarnation;
- a Role occupied across contexts;
- a transmitted influence;
- a remembered or anticipated presence;
- or another kind of continuity.

Presence is anchored. Mechanism remains open.

### A-006 — Mirrored potentiality

Efiishent and Vitriol are distinct Characters operating from mirrored potentiality.

The relationship remains a live question. Its ultimate meaning is not fixed.

### A-007 — Seven timelines are deferred

Seven timelines will later be considered across the three era constellations.

Version 0.1 does not map, number or assign those timelines.

Existing seven-phase or seven-timeline material is retained as source material but is not silently imported into this macro structure.
