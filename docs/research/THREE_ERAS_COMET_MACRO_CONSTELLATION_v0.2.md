## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the current outer boundary of the Future Sky narrative field.

The macro structure contains:

- one long-period comet;
- three era encounters approximately 100,000 years apart;
- three names given to the comet by the peoples of those eras;
- one fixed Jyotzon history;
- seven Omincron timelines, A–G;
- one fixed Manzo history;
- efiishent and Lord Vitriol present throughout the larger structure;
- the Cube Loop linking Omincron and Jyotzon;
- a second retrocausal loop linking Omincron and Manzo.

The detailed narrative of Timeline G is recorded separately. This document defines the shared field without pretending that every causal relationship is resolved.

## 2. Primary correction to v0.1

There are not three unrelated comets.

There is **one comet**, encountered and named differently in three eras:

| Era | Approximate date | Era-name for the comet | Timeline topology |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Jyotzon | c. 98,000 BCE | Jyotzon | one fixed timeline |
| Omincron | 2019 CE encounter | Omincron | seven timelines, A–G |
| Manzo | c. 102,019 CE | Manzo | one fixed timeline |

The intervals are approximate. The comet has a nominal orbital period of roughly 100,000 years; fragmentation, close encounters and intervention may perturb its exact return.

The comet is the physical continuity joining the three eras. Its identity does not depend upon any civilisation recognising that continuity.

## 4. Current macro Anchors

### A-001 — One comet

The Jyotzon, Omincron and Manzo comets are the same celestial body.

### A-002 — Three names

Each era names and understands the comet from within its own cultural and epistemic context.

No era is assumed to possess a complete account of the comet's origin, life-carrying function or larger cosmic role.

### A-003 — Approximate 100,000-year cadence

The comet returns to the inner Solar System on a nominal period of approximately 100,000 years.

This cadence is scientifically plausible for a long-period comet. Exact symmetry is not required.

### A-004 — Asymmetric timeline topology

```text
Jyotzon:  one fixed timeline
Omincron: seven timelines — A, B, C, D, E, F and G
Manzo:    one fixed timeline
```

The relationship between the fixed eras and the seven Omincron lines remains partly unresolved.

### A-005 — Cross-context efiishent

efiishent is present within Jyotzon, Omincron and Manzo.

Presence does not imply omniscience, perfect memory or a single uncomplicated continuity of self.

### A-006 — Cross-context Vitriol

Lord Vitriol is present throughout the larger drama.

His continuity may involve one consciousness, versions, incarnations, Roles or another mechanism not yet resolved.

### A-007 — Mirrored potentiality

efiishent and Vitriol are distinct Characters operating from mirrored potentiality.

Neither is reducible to the other. Their ultimate meaning remains a live question.

### A-008 — Neptune Lounge continuity

The ancient Jyotzon temple, the Omincron dance floor and the Manzo Neptune Lounge occupy a persistent narrative place.

Physical continuity, symbolic continuity and Anchor continuity must not be assumed to be identical.

### A-009 — Causation need not follow experienced time

Within the Broken Sky field, an event may occur before all of its causes have occurred in the lived chronology of its participants.

The past is not casually rewritten. Later actions may close a causal structure already expressed in an earlier event.

## 5. Jyotzon Era constellation

**Approximate date:** c. 98,000 BCE  
**Comet name:** Jyotzon  
**Timeline topology:** one fixed history  
**Earth condition:** recognisably Earth; potentially colder, drier and shaped by a different glacial and coastal configuration

### 5.1 Comet crisis

The Jyotzon comet is on a collision course with Earth.

During the months before its arrival, ancient people perform an increasingly powerful ritual calling for divine intervention.

The ritual opens a portal.

### 5.2 Arrival of efiishent

efiishent is ripped unwillingly from his life in Omincron while dancing with an ordinary CMYK Cube.

He arrives:

- in the Jyotzon era;
- at the ancient temple occupying the future site of Neptune Lounge;
- disoriented and without complete knowledge;
- carrying the Cube he had been handling on the dance floor.

The Jyotzon people may understand his arrival as an answer to their ritual. This does not establish that they intentionally summoned him as a known individual.

### 5.3 Encoding and launch of the Cube

A plan is developed to turn the ordinary Cube into a time capsule.

Through ritual and ancient technology, the Cube is encoded with mysteries, knowledge and traces of Jyotzon.

It is then launched into a precisely calculated orbit so that it returns to Earth during Omincron.

The Cube travels into the future physically. Its informational significance is created recursively through its passage around the loop.

### 5.4 Vitriol's intervention

Vitriol understands enough of the Cube's purpose to attempt to prevent it leaving Earth.

His precise knowledge, motive and relationship to the comet remain unresolved.

### 5.5 The impact

The catastrophe cannot be completely prevented.

A working physical model is:

- a roughly one-to-two-kilometre fragment separates from the primary comet;
- the fragment strikes Earth, probably near the Jyotzon heartland or a shallow coastal sea;
- the primary comet survives and continues its long orbit;
- the impact destroys Jyotzon civilisation;
- tsunamis, fire, atmospheric debris and several years of climatic disruption produce global consequences;
- scattered human populations survive.

This is a civilisational collapse and bottleneck, not a species-ending mass extinction.

### 5.6 Long recovery

Earth remains habitable.

A plausible recovery field is:

- immediate survival and famine across the first decade;
- stabilisation of scattered cultures over generations;
- regional societies and agriculture re-emerging over centuries or millennia;
- multiple later civilisational cycles during the next 100,000 years;
- reliable knowledge of Jyotzon almost entirely disappearing.

Jyotzon technology should not yet be treated as a planet-wide industrial system. Its sophistication may be ritual, cosmological, computational, ecological or materially light.

The Cube becomes one of the few deliberately transmitted Jyotzon records.

## 6. Omincron Era constellation

**Primary encounter:** 2019 CE  
**Timeline topology:** seven lines, A–G  
**Detailed line currently available:** Timeline G  
**Era expression:** the Fracturing Now

### 6.1 The Cube in Timeline G

The encoded Cube arrives in 2007. efiishent unknowingly films its arrival:

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dmedxQF2HQ>

He finds the Cube in 2020 and realises that the earlier recording appears to contain its arrival.

He frequently loses and rediscovers it.

Possession of the Cube is therefore discontinuous even though the Cube's role persists.

### 6.2 The Omincron encounter

In 2019, Omincron approaches Earth on a collision trajectory.

Vitriol has been spreading a virus that effectively turns human beings into magnets, increasing humanity's attraction of the comet toward Earth.

Whether "magnet" is electromagnetic, biological, psychic, symbolic or a combination remains open at this research stage.

### 6.3 The planetary intervention

A powerful ritual links nodes of human beings across the planet.

Many participants do not consciously understand the ceremony. They experience:

- psychedelic and physical intensity;
- connection with distant participants;
- connection through Earth;
- a massive rush of energy entering from beyond the planet;
- collective participation in a minute alteration of Earth's orbit.

The micro-shift allows Earth to avoid direct collision.

Vitriol's mission fails in Timeline G because of the heroes' intervention.

### 6.4 The Broken Sky

Omincron's passage breaks the sky and fractures timelines.

Most people cannot perceive the damage. Those who participated in the intervention can.

efiishent does not know how many others experienced it, but understands that he must find them.

Some are found through adventures surrounding the game he builds. Others locate him by their own means.

The game functions as a recognition instrument as well as a created world.

### 6.5 The 2025 Jyotzon passage

In 2025, efiishent enters the portal and lives for a time in Jyotzon.

He later returns to Omincron gifted—or cursed—with knowledge of past and future.

He sometimes questions his sanity. Certain truths nevertheless continue to present themselves.

The research model must preserve both realities:

- efiishent has encountered genuine inaccessible truths;
- his interpretations remain partial, human and fallible.

### 6.6 Awakening and passage to Manzo

As the world descends into chaos, seven future scenarios loom.

Through the Cube and powers latent within themselves, participants uncover paths into the Broken Sky through:

- ritual;
- ceremony;
- dance;
- psytrance and other underground music;
- individual discovery;
- cohort experience.

They enter portals leading toward Manzo.

## 7. The Cube Loop

```text
Omincron 2025
efiishent carries an ordinary CMYK Cube into Jyotzon
        ↓
Jyotzon
the Cube is encoded and launched into a long orbit
        ↓
Omincron 2007
the encoded Cube arrives and is unknowingly filmed
        ↓
Omincron 2020
efiishent finds and recognises it
        ↓
Omincron 2025
he carries it into Jyotzon
```

The loop does not need to create the Cube's physical matter.

Its manufacture may occur conventionally in Omincron. What is generated recursively is its meaning, encoded knowledge and causal role.

Open questions include:

- who manufactured the initially ordinary Cube;
- how it is launched and survives;
- what "precisely" guides its return;
- whether every Omincron timeline contains the same Cube Loop;
- whether losing and refinding it protects the loop;
- whether Vitriol can damage the knowledge without destroying the object.

## 8. The Broken Sky Loop

Timeline G contains a second loop:

```text
the 2019 intervention succeeds
        ↓
participants survive, awaken and find one another
        ↓
they enter the Broken Sky portals
        ↓
they reach Manzo
        ↓
their Manzo quest succeeds
        ↓
that success enables the 2019 intervention to succeed
```

Working term: **retrocausal closure**.

The 2019 event remains an immutable fact within Timeline G. Later success in Manzo completes its causation rather than editing a settled event.

This distinction should be protected in any future runtime representation:

- event occurrence;
- participant observation;
- recorded history;
- discovered causal relationship;
- loop closure;

are separate layers.

## 9. Manzo Era constellation

**Approximate date:** c. 102,019 CE  
**Comet name:** Manzo  
**Timeline topology:** one fixed history  
**Primary region:** Triton Central  
**Primary place:** Neptune Lounge  
**Development status:** emerging macro outline

### 9.1 Anticipated arrival

The Manzo comet's return is known and long anticipated.

Unlike Jyotzon's desperation and Omincron's near-catastrophe, Manzo's arrival is approached as a celebration.

Many parties are organised across the Earth system.

The comet remains powerful within the psyche, cultures and histories of Manzo-era inhabitants.

### 9.2 The accelerated opening

Human science, technology, creativity and morality accelerate greatly during Omincron.

The first two centuries after Omincron's passage contain multiple quantum leaps that open humanity toward the larger cosmos.

This does not require uninterrupted progress. The path may include collapse, conflict, rediscovery and moral struggle.

By Manzo:

- the Earth system supports deep, layered civilisation;
- Triton Central is culturally and technologically mature;
- Neptune Lounge has become a major living node;
- humanity participates in an interstellar context;
- the returning comet is remembered through science, myth, ritual, entertainment and contested history.

### 9.3 Celebratory texture

Manzo's comet encounter may contain:

- system-wide festivals;
- pilgrimages;
- scientific observation;
- ritual preparation;
- commercial spectacle;
- underground gatherings;
- competing historical interpretations;
- parties whose participants do not agree on what is being celebrated;
- Earth chips as a celebrated interstellar cultural export.

Celebration does not guarantee safety.

The precise purpose of the Manzo quest, what is being "caught," and how its success closes the Omincron intervention remain to be authored.

## 10. Earth across the 200,000-year field

Earth remains geologically recognisable across the full span.

Over 100,000 years:

- plates may move kilometres rather than rearranging the entire planet;
- glacial cycles may radically move coastlines;
- sea level may vary by more than one hundred metres;
- local landscapes, rivers, islands and settlements may disappear;
- humans can adapt through migration, culture and technology.

The Jyotzon impact is the principal discontinuity.

Ordinary geological and climatic change does not prevent human persistence. It prevents any civilisation from treating its current geography as permanent.

## 11. Omincron's seven timelines

The seven timelines belong specifically to Omincron:

```text
Omincron A
Omincron B
Omincron C
Omincron D
Omincron E
Omincron F
Omincron G
```

Timeline G is presently the best-formed line and the first detailed case.

Its existence may itself be a macro Anchor. That does not make it:

- original;
- correct;
- privileged;
- least corrupted;
- fully recoverable;
- fully understood.

The seven lines may be existing parallel histories, future attractors made visible through the Broken Sky, or a structure combining both. This remains open.

Jyotzon and Manzo remain fixed to one timeline each. Whether all Omincron lines share those fixed boundaries is unresolved.
