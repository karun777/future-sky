## 1. Purpose

Timeline G is the first Omincron timeline with substantial authored narrative.

It is:

- narratively rich;
- deliberately obfuscated;
- partially inaccessible to its own participants;
- a candidate macro Anchor;
- the line in which Vitriol's Omincron plan fails through collective intervention.

This document records what has been established without declaring Timeline G the original or correct history.

## 4. The Cube Loop

```text
ordinary Cube in Omincron
        ↓ carried through the 2025 portal
encoded Cube in Jyotzon
        ↓ placed into a precise physical orbit
arrival in Omincron 2007
        ↓ found and recognised in 2020
carried into Jyotzon in 2025
```

### Protected distinctions

- The Cube's matter may have an ordinary manufacture.
- Its encoded knowledge originates in Jyotzon.
- Its significance is recursively produced.
- Its 2007 arrival and 2020 discovery are different events.
- efiishent's possession is discontinuous.
- The Cube's identity must not depend solely upon continuous custody.

## 5. The 2019 intervention

Vitriol spreads a virus that effectively turns human beings into magnets, attempting to attract Omincron toward Earth and destroy terrestrial life.

A distributed ritual links human nodes across the planet.

Participants may be unaware of the ceremony's full scale. They experience connection across distance and through Earth, accompanied by psychedelic and physical intensity.

Energy arriving from beyond Earth enables a minute orbital shift.

The intervention succeeds in Timeline G.

Omincron passes close enough to break the sky.

## 6. Perception after the fracture

Most humans cannot see the Broken Sky.

Participants in the ritual can perceive it to differing degrees.

efiishent:

- knows others participated;
- does not know how many;
- feels compelled to find them;
- builds a game that becomes one means of recognition;
- is also found by participants following their own paths;
- questions his sanity;
- continues encountering truths that resist dismissal.

Genuine revelation does not eliminate incomplete interpretation.

## 8. Retrocausal closure

Timeline G's second loop is:

```text
2019 avoidance
    → survival and gathering
    → Broken Sky passage
    → Manzo quest
    → Manzo success
    → causal completion of 2019 avoidance
```

Working proposition:

> The heroes can achieve the 2019 intervention only because they later travel through the Broken Sky to Manzo and succeed there.

The past is contingent upon success after it.

This does not require a visible rewrite of 2019. Manzo success may close a cause that had already acted across fractured time.

## 9. Candidate Anchor status

A working proposition remains:

```text
Timeline G must exist for the larger Omincron field to retain its intended structure.
```

This concerns existence, not moral or ontological supremacy.

Timeline G may be:

- one inhabited line among seven;
- one future attractor that becomes historical;
- the successful closure of the Cube and Broken Sky loops;
- a protected or imprisoned line;
- all of these in different senses.

No selection is made yet.
