# LAWS.md
## The Fundamental Laws of Future Sky

Status: Draft v0.1

---

# Purpose

Everything in Future Sky should ultimately be explainable through a small number of universal laws.

These are not programming rules.

They are the natural laws of the universe.

Every mechanic, story, room, system and timeline should eventually be understood as an expression of one or more of these laws.

---

# Law I — Arrival

Consciousness always arrives.

Nobody begins with complete understanding.

Every player awakens.

Arrival is mystery.

Not explanation.

---

# Law II — Reset

Consciousness always returns to an Anchor.

Reset is not death.

Reset is not punishment.

Reset is continuation.

The world quietly carries consciousness between awakenings.

---

# Law III — World Moments

The world never waits.

Events occur with or without witnesses.

Players experience moments.

The world experiences itself.

Rooms are collections of possible moments.

---

# Law IV — Memory

Memory is earned.

Memory may be hidden.

Memory may be altered.

Memory may be shared.

Memory itself is gameplay.

Knowledge is one of the world's currencies.

---

# Law V — Resonance

Everything resonates.

Objects.

Rooms.

People.

Music.

Ideas.

Timelines.

Resonance creates connection.

Connection creates change.

---

# Law VI — The Cube

The Cube exists across every era.

It remembers.

It observes.

It connects.

The Cube is not merely an object.

It is a cosmological constant.

---

# Law VII — The Comets

Comets disturb reality.

They alter probability.

They weaken boundaries between timelines.

Comets are not disasters.

They are opportunities for transformation.

---

# Law VIII — Participation

The player is never merely an observer.

Observation changes reality.

Choice changes reality.

Silence changes reality.

Participation is the primary mechanic of Future Sky.

---

# Law IX — Authorship

Everything has an author.

Rooms.

Moments.

Music.

Images.

Stories.

Future Sky remembers those who contribute to its becoming.

The Archive preserves authorship.

---

# Law X — The Archive

Nothing is truly lost.

Every memory.

Every image.

Every song.

Every story.

Every contributor.

The Archive remembers.

---

# Law XI — Living Worlds

The world is not static.

NPCs continue living.

Rooms continue changing.

Music continues playing.

The world does not freeze while players are absent.

Reality continues.

---

# Law XII — The Code is Love

The Code is Love.

Love is not sentiment.

Love is participation.

Love is curiosity.

Love is generosity.

Love is coherence.

Love is the force that allows consciousness to become civilisation.

Every system should ultimately move toward greater coherence.

---

# Design Test

Whenever a new mechanic is proposed, ask:

Does it express one or more of the Laws?

If not...

Why does it exist?

---

# Long-term Vision

Future Sky should eventually become simple enough that almost every mechanic can be traced back to these laws.

The complexity belongs in their interaction.

Not in the number of systems.

A civilisation is easier to understand when its laws are elegant.
