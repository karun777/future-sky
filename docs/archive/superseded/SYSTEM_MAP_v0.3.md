# Purpose

SYSTEM_MAP defines the ontology of Future Sky.

It identifies what exists, what categories of truth exist, and which
authorities steward those truths.

It does **not** define implementation details.

Those belong to the Runtime Authority Register and implementation
documents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Constitutional Position

Future Sky is organised as a constitutional hierarchy.

``` text
Reality
    ↓
Natural Laws
    ↓
Pramá
    ↓
Social Cycle
    ↓
Civilisation
    ↓
Authorities
    ↓
Events
    ↓
Perception
    ↓
Expression
```

Natural Laws describe why reality moves.

Pramá describes how movement remains coherent.

The Social Cycle describes one constitutional pattern through which
collective power moves.

SYSTEM_MAP describes how these principles become a structured universe.

# Layers of Reality

## 1. Cosmology

Owns universal law, origins, metaphysical structure and the deepest
constants.

## 2. Timelines

Owns historical continuity, eras, branching and convergence.

## 3. Worlds

Owns planetary and world-scale simulation.

## 4. Civilisation and Social Power

Owns institutions, factions, governance, legitimacy, social
organisation, collective memory, and civilisational transition.

Civilisation Authority **does not determine Pramá**.

It publishes civilisational truth that contributes to constitutional
evaluation.

## 5. Locations

Owns local reality.

## 6. Beings

Owns identity, capabilities, memory, relationships and persistent
personal state.

## 7. Moments

Owns temporary situations including Scenarios, Thread Nodes,
conversations, rituals and encounters.

## 8. Perception

Owns subjective awareness, knowledge visibility and interpretation.

## 9. Expression

Owns presentation through Discord, websites, Feed7, the companion app
and future interfaces.

# Authorities

Authorities own one category of truth.

Authorities may:

-   validate
-   mutate
-   persist
-   publish events

Authorities never silently rewrite another authority's truth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Fields

Fields are **distributed conditions**.

They are not authorities.

They describe tendencies, pressures and environmental conditions
distributed across the universe.

Examples include:

-   resonance
-   portal pressure
-   institutional legitimacy
-   exploitation pressure
-   timeline stability
-   social cohesion

Authorities publish truth.

Fields describe changing conditions.

Fields do not decide outcomes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Pramá

Pramá is **not** a runtime authority.

It is not a field.

It is not a simulation score.

Pramá is the constitutional evaluation of whether diverse systems remain
capable of continued coordinated development.

Pramá observes the accumulated consequences of many authorities.

Future runtime evaluators may derive Pramá-related observations from
authoritative state, but no single subsystem owns or writes "Pramá."

The detailed constitutional model belongs in `PRAMA_MODEL.md`.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Runtime Relationship

The runtime is an implementation of this ontology.

Runtime authorities steward the truths defined here.

Events communicate completed facts between authorities.

Presentation layers reveal those truths without owning them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stewardship

Future Sky should evolve by extending existing authorities before
creating new ones.

Implementation may change completely.

Ontology should change rarely.

Constitutional principles should change only with great care.
