# Future Sky --- TRAJECTORY v2.1

**Status:** Canonical Constitution\
**Version:** 2.1 (Draft)\
**Purpose:** Describe what Future Sky is becoming.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Future Sky is not being built from mechanics upward.

Nor is it being built from stories downward.

It is being built through the emergence of civilization itself.

Mechanics, stories, institutions, cultures and technologies are all
expressions of a deeper process:

**Consciousness becoming relationship.\
Relationship becoming community.\
Community becoming civilization.\
Civilization attempting to remember itself through the continual
relationship between consciousness, memory, time and meaning.**

Future Sky does not seek to simulate every possibility.

**It seeks to construct a world in which meaningful possibilities can
continuously emerge.**

This document is not an operational manual.

It is not a technical specification.

It exists to answer one question:

> **What is Future Sky becoming?**

When uncertainty arises, this document takes precedence over temporary
implementation decisions.

# Core Premise

Future Sky is a simulation of a civilization whose primary medium is
**meaning rather than mechanics**.

Complexity should emerge.

Never be skipped.

Never be faked.

The architecture should create conditions from which living worlds
naturally arise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Constitutional Invariants

Every future subsystem should preserve these principles.

-   Coherence precedes complexity.
-   Emergence is preferred over prescription.
-   Memory precedes meaning.
-   Relationship precedes optimisation.
-   Human participation is preferred but never required.
-   Observation precedes intervention.
-   Stewardship is the mature expression of capability.
-   Every era possesses its own experience of time.
-   Every system contributes observations before it contributes
    outcomes.

# The Temporal Spine

Future Sky does not contain one universal experience of time.

Different beings inhabit different temporal realities.

    Moment
        ↓
    Memory
        ↓
    History
        ↓
    Civilization
        ↓
    Myth
        ↓
    Cycle
        ↓
    Renewal

Every Era possesses its own temporal grammar.

-   **Jyotzon** experiences time as urgency.
-   **Manzo** experiences time as recurrence, fragmentation and
    rediscovery.
-   **Ominicron** experiences time as inheritance and consequence.

Likewise:

-   Players
-   Characters
-   NPCs
-   Institutions
-   Factions
-   Civilizations
-   Comets
-   The Cube

may all experience time differently while inhabiting the same world.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Observation

Future Sky prefers worlds that notice themselves before worlds that
constantly change themselves.

Subsystems should emit observations before attempting intervention.

Meaning emerges through the convergence of many observations rather than
through centralised orchestration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Chapters, Moments, Scenarios and Thread Nodes

    Chapter
        ↓
    Moment
        ↓
    Scenario
        ↓
    Thread Node

**Chapters** describe developmental contexts.

**Moments** are emergent conditions in the world.

Moments are not authored directly.

They arise when independent conditions become coherent.

**Scenarios** are bounded living situations.

They coordinate participants.

**Thread Nodes** remain the primary unit of explicit agency.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# World Moments

World Moments communicate that the world continues independently of the
player.

They may include:

-   environmental change
-   rumours
-   broadcasts
-   artwork
-   NPC behaviour
-   music
-   Cube resonance
-   weather

They communicate life rather than exposition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Human Participation

The world always prefers genuine human participation.

The world never depends upon it.

Continuity may be maintained by:

-   Human
-   AI
-   Script
-   Dormant

The Role persists regardless of the Driver.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Emergence

Future Sky is intentionally designed so that authored content is
multiplied rather than consumed.

Rooms.

Roles.

Stories.

Institutions.

Objects.

Relationships.

Should participate in many different futures.

The architecture values meaningful recombination over endless expansion.

Civilization should gradually become capable of generating history that
no author explicitly wrote.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stewardship

The highest expression of participation is stewardship.

Ownership asks:

> What belongs to me?

Stewardship asks:

> What has been entrusted to my care?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# LOVE

LOVE is not a reward.

LOVE is not a mechanic.

LOVE is orientation.

Players may first encounter LOVE as:

-   a mystery
-   a code
-   a phrase
-   a civilisation

Eventually they discover that LOVE has quietly organised the
architecture from the beginning.

It is never enforced.

Only discovered.
