# RESET.md
## Future Sky Reset System

Status: Draft v0.1

---

# Philosophy

Reset is not a respawn mechanic.

Reset is a natural law of the Future Sky universe.

Consciousness does not simply appear.

It awakens.

It rests.

It returns.

Reset is the mechanism by which consciousness continues its journey.

---

# Design Principles

Reset should:

- respect the player's real life
- never punish absence
- preserve immersion
- feel natural
- support narrative
- support future mechanics

The world should quietly care for the player while they are away.

---

# Core Concept

Every character possesses a current Reset Anchor.

When a reset occurs, consciousness returns to that anchor.

Initially this will always be:

Pod Room

As the game develops, players unlock additional anchors.

Examples:

- Pod
- Hostel Bed
- Camp Site
- Meditation Chamber
- Cube Chamber
- Home
- Safe House
- Dream Sanctuary

---

# Reset Triggers

Examples include:

## Arrival

The player's first awakening.

---

## Idle

Player has been inactive long enough.

The character quietly returns to their Reset Anchor.

---

## Death

The body fails.

Consciousness returns elsewhere.

Inventory, memories or timeline state may change.

---

## Dream

The player awakens somewhere unexpected.

---

## Timeline

Reality reorganises itself.

---

## Comet

Large-scale cosmological reset.

---

## Manual

Future ability allowing voluntary meditation or rest.

---

# Reset Types

## Soft Reset

No penalties.

Examples:

- Idle
- Rest
- Sleep

---

## Narrative Reset

Story advances.

Examples:

- Dream
- Memory fragment
- Thread completion

---

## Mechanical Reset

Gameplay consequences.

Examples:

- Death
- Timeline collapse

---

## Cosmological Reset

World-scale changes.

Examples:

- Comets
- Era shifts
- Timeline convergence

---

# Reset Consequences

A reset may alter:

- location
- inventory
- memories
- NPC relationships
- room state
- timeline
- statistics
- quests
- active thread nodes

Or...

Nothing at all.

# Memory

Reset does not imply forgetting.

Reset changes consciousness.

Memory itself becomes a gameplay system.

Possible future mechanics include:

- forgotten memories
- recovered memories
- false memories
- shared memories
- Cube echoes

# Relationship to Arrival

Arrival is the first Reset.

The player awakens.

Every subsequent Reset is another awakening.

The only difference is what has changed.

---

# Long-term Vision

Reset becomes one of the fundamental laws of Future Sky.

Players will eventually understand that:

Nobody truly disappears.

Nobody simply respawns.

Consciousness always finds its way back to an anchor.

Sometimes unchanged.

Sometimes profoundly different.
