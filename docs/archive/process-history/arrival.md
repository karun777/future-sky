## Stage of Journey

Players are in Chiang Mai.  Or Fremantle.  Or wherever.  They exist where they exist.  

A *character*, *another being* with whom they exist in unique parallelism with has come to life in a pod inside the Neptune Lounge.

The shared resonance across time and space triggerd a portal opening between the player and the character.  The exact particulars of how this event horizon opened is worth examining for each player but can only ultimately be explained by mystery. There was a wizard involved somewhere in the process. One that occasionally shares their timeline.  A silly one.  A confused one.  A highly autistic one.

Neither the player or their character has any information or memory as to their purpose.  The character is waking with many latent faculties and with fragments of memories that cannot as yet be accessed.  There is a sense of them though and once they return they can be mostly trusted.

## Visible Narrative (What the Player Experiences)

### What does the player actually see?

Waking up, regaining consciousness, the player looks first to their hands.  Unfamiliar.  Who's hands are these?

They feel something on their chest.  Notice their breathing.  The air smells something...almost familiar.  Earthy?  Fresh despite the small space.

They notice they are in a pod.  A square pod.  Cube shaped.  They are lying down connected to some apparatus.  How exactly is unclear.  No needles or anything physical that can be observed.  There is some kind of hum they can feel around their skin.  Inside them somehow.

It feels warm and comforting yet at the same time intense.  A scan runs through.  Light and sound and vibration penetrating and caressing.  Weird.

The scan finishes. They can move the rest of their body.  They hadn't noticed they couldn't do that until then.

Coherence 97% Parallelism 78% appears in HUD.

They notice that they have a HUD.  It's somehow embedded in their vision.  Other vital statistics flicker and seem important but it's going to take some getting used to having a display unit in your field of vision.

It is pushed into the background as they, moving to a sitting positon, feet hanging over the pod bed take a further look at their surrounding.

It feels like a little living cubicle. Spartan. Functional. But comfortable. Cozy. Wizardy. High-tech.

There is a display port with a feed from something called the neptune lounge.  as their focus sharpens on the screen an option appears inside the character's HUD with instructions to execute command !feed7 to view

Feed7 opens fullscreen.  Closing feed returns player to pod.

Noise can be heard outside the pod.  Faint but clearly audible.  A feeling of movement.

An announcement.  "Your pod is cleared for exit".  Gentle yet implying haste.

"Prepare for coherence test.  Stand when ready?".

Choices flash in HUD.  Text options...

Explore pod further.

Look for some food.
Go back to sleep.
Stand.
Look back to feed7.

player stands. it is inevitable.  movement is a must for all. stars, nebulae, planets, humans - all are on the move
