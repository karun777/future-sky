# Future Sky – CHANGELOG v1.0

## Purpose

This document records the major developmental milestones in the evolution of Future Sky.

It is not intended to capture every bug fix, command addition, refactor, or implementation detail.

Those belong in source control.

This document records moments where Future Sky changed direction.

Moments where architecture, philosophy, onboarding, world structure, or developmental understanding shifted in a meaningful way.

The purpose of this document is to answer:

> How did Future Sky become what it is?

---

# Changelog Philosophy

Future Sky is not being built as a conventional software product.

It is being built as an evolving world.

As such, the most important changes are not always technical.

A new onboarding rite may be more significant than a thousand lines of code.

A new understanding of memory may matter more than a database migration.

This changelog therefore prioritises:

- Architectural milestones
- Philosophical milestones
- World milestones
- Infrastructure milestones
- Public milestones

over implementation details.

---

# Canon Events

The following events are considered foundational turning points.

---

## 2026-01-05

### Presence becomes first-class architecture

Future Sky adopts the concept of Presence as a core design principle.

The world begins treating player attention, activity, absence and return as meaningful states rather than technical conditions.

This eventually develops into:

- Active Presence
- Idle Presence
- Haunting Presence

and influences nearly every future system.

---

## 2026-01-15

### Time becomes a first-class system

Time ceases to be a background mechanic.

Future Sky begins treating time as a living force.

This lays the foundation for:

- World clocks
- Era progression
- Ambient systems
- Comet cycles
- Temporal pressure

---

## 2026-06-05

### Internal Narrator recognised as core architecture

The Internal Narrator evolves from an interesting feature idea into a foundational design pillar.

Future memory systems, dream sequences, flashbacks, telepathic communication, narrative guidance and personal reflection systems become unified under a single conceptual framework.

---

## 2026-06-19

### The Arrival Rite becomes canonical

The onboarding sequence:

Pod
↓
Signal
↓
Chamber
↓
LOVE
↓
Neptune Lounge

is established as the intended initiation path into Future Sky.

Arrival becomes ceremony rather than account creation.

# Chronological Record

---

# January 2026

## Foundations

### 2026-01-04

Future Sky transitions from a primarily written concept into an operational multiplayer world.

Major developments include:

- Run channels stabilised
- Shared presence introduced
- Social interaction expanded
- Return Echoes established
- Atmospheric systems strengthened

The world becomes inhabited.

---

### 2026-01-04

Command discovery emerges as a design direction.

Future Sky begins moving away from exposing all capabilities immediately.

The concept that abilities should be discovered rather than granted begins forming.

This later evolves into the Capability Spine.

---

# January 2026

## Presence

### 2026-01-05

The Active → Idle → Haunting model is introduced.

Presence becomes architecture rather than flavour.

The world begins responding to player attention rather than simply player actions.

---

### 2026-01-05

Soft Time, Sharp Time and Critical Time are formalised.

Future Sky begins exploring temporal pressure as a design tool.

---

# January 2026

## Manuscripts

### 2026-01-12

Core Future Sky manuscript work reaches a significant level of completion.

Key worldbuilding documents stabilise.

An important implication emerges:

Future Sky exists independently of any specific implementation.

The Discord world becomes one manifestation of Future Sky rather than its origin.

---

# January 2026

## Architecture

### 2026-01-08

DEV_STATE emerges.

Future Sky begins maintaining an operational understanding of itself.

The project starts behaving like a living system rather than a collection of files.

---

### 2026-01-19

JSON authority becomes formalised.

State ownership, persistence boundaries and operational responsibilities become clearer.

This period marks the beginning of architectural stabilisation.

The kingdom gains a constitution.

---

# January 2026

## Time

### 2026-01-15

World Time architecture is expanded.

Temporal systems become increasingly integrated into the identity of the world.

This eventually contributes to:

- Era architecture
- World clocks
- Temporal persistence
- Comet cycle thinking

# June 2026

### 2026-06-03

Future Sky becomes publicly accessible through HTTPS infrastructure.

---

### 2026-06-03

Future Sky Node planning begins in earnest.

The concept of physical discovery nodes becomes an active development priority.

Chiang Mai emerges as the first active public node.

---

The Internal Narrator is promoted to a core architectural system.
