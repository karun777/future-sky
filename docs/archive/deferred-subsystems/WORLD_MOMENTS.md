## Future Sky World Moments

Status: Draft v0.1

---

## Philosophy

The world should not feel like it is waiting for the player.

The world is alive.

Players witness moments.

Some moments involve them.

Some do not.

---

## What is a World Moment?

A World Moment is any event that the world decides to present.

It may be:

- subtle
- dramatic
- visual
- audible
- narrative
- mechanical

A World Moment is not simply text.

It is an opportunity for the world to breathe.

---

## Examples

- A distant laugh.
- The DJ changes track.
- The lights dim.
- The Cube whispers.
- Someone drops a feather.
- The Dark Rat Queen walks past.
- Butterflies fill the room.
- Neptune flashes with lightning.
- A memory returns.
- The room falls silent.

---

## A World Moment may contain

- text
- artwork
- soundtrack
- sound effect
- NPC appearance
- inventory change
- room state change
- thread node trigger
- internal narration
- HUD notification
- animation (future)
- video (future)

Any combination is valid.

---

## A World Moment is authored.

A moment may credit:

- writer
- visual artist
- musician
- curator
- programmer

Future Sky is collaborative.

The engine should preserve authorship.

---

## Relationship to the Renderer

The renderer does not generate moments.

The renderer presents moments.

The World Moment engine decides *what* happens.

The Renderer decides *how* it is experienced.

---

## Relationship to Rooms

Rooms are collections of possible moments.

Two players may enter the same room.

Neither is guaranteed to experience the same sequence.

---

## Relationship to Reset

Reset is a World Moment.

Arrival is a World Moment.

Dreams are World Moments.

Death is a World Moment.

Comets are World Moments.

The distinction exists only in narrative and mechanics.

---

## Long-term Goal

Future Sky is not a sequence of rooms.

It is a sequence of moments.

The player remembers moments.

The rooms simply provide the stage.
