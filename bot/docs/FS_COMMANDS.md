# Future Sky — Commands

Core:
- `!look` — describe your current location
- `!go <direction>` — move (e.g. `!go north`)
- `!sneak` — move quietly (currently always succeeds)
- `!attack <enemy>` — fight something in the room

Social / vibe:
- `!say <message>` — speak to others in the room (if enabled)
- `!emote <action>` — emote (if enabled)

Notes:
- You can type commands anywhere, but responses route to your run channel.
- The Realm Feed is for world-scale signals, not individual actions.
