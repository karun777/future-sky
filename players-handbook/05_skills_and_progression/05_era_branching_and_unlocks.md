# Era Branching and Unlocks

Future Sky unfolds across multiple eras.

While the core of a character persists, the world they inhabit changes — technologically, culturally, and cosmologically. Era branching formalises how those changes shape progression without fragmenting identity.

This chapter explains how skills, abilities, and capabilities unlock differently across eras.

---

## One Character, Many Contexts

Characters do not reset between eras.

They carry:
- Memory
- Identity
- Core capabilities
- Lingering strain and resonance

What changes is *how* those capabilities can be expressed.

---

## Era as Constraint and Opportunity

Each era introduces:
- New tools and technologies
- Different cultural norms
- Altered risks and pressures
- Distinct environmental challenges
- Shifts in what mastery looks like

An era does not make a character stronger.
It changes the terrain on which strength is applied.

---

## Branching, Not Replacement

Era branching does not replace progression.

Instead, it:
- Unlocks new expressions of existing skills
- Restricts or modifies others
- Introduces era-specific techniques
- Alters cost structures and risks

A skill does not disappear.
Its expression adapts.

---

## Unlock Conditions

Era-specific unlocks may require:
- Reaching a proficiency threshold
- Experiencing a key event
- Completing a narrative milestone
- Accessing specific knowledge or technology
- Achieving chakra or ability alignment

Unlocks are earned, not automatic.

---

## Examples of Era Expression

The same underlying skill may manifest differently:

- **Navigation**
  - Star charts and intuition
  - GPS and satellite data
  - Psychic mapping and temporal echoes

- **Engineering**
  - Mechanical construction
  - Networked systems
  - Bio-tech or mythic devices

- **Influence**
  - Oral tradition and ritual
  - Media and mass communication
  - Resonant or memetic effect

The skill remains.
The method evolves.

---

## Locked and Dormant Capabilities

Some capabilities may be:
- Temporarily inaccessible
- Dormant due to context
- Illegal or taboo
- Technologically unsupported

These limitations are not punishments.
They are part of the world’s logic.

---

## Cross-Era Resonance

Certain developments echo across eras.

A breakthrough in one era may:
- Simplify learning in another
- Reduce cost for aligned actions
- Unlock recognition or authority
- Create narrative callbacks

The world remembers what you’ve learned.

---

## Avoiding Fragmentation

Era branching is designed to preserve coherence.

Characters should:
- Feel continuous
- Recognise themselves across contexts
- Carry familiar strengths and weaknesses

If a character feels like a different person, something has gone wrong.

---

## Why This Matters

This approach ensures that:
- Long-term play remains fresh
- Progression respects narrative continuity
- Era shifts feel meaningful
- Players are rewarded for sustained investment
- The system scales without bloating

You grow *with* the world, not against it.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “What do I lose in this era?”

Ask instead:
- “What becomes possible now?”
- “What must I relearn?”
- “What parts of me persist?”

The era changes.

You remain.
