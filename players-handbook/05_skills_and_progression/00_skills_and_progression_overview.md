# Skills and Progression — Overview

Skills are how characters *touch the world*.

Progression is how those touches change over time.

In Future Sky, skills and progression are not abstract ladders. They are records of use, pressure, failure, training, and adaptation. This section explains how characters learn, improve, specialise, and change — without locking growth into rigid tracks.

---

## What Skills Represent

A skill represents applied capability.

It reflects:
- Experience under pressure
- Learned technique
- Familiarity with tools or contexts
- Pattern recognition
- Confidence earned through repetition

Skills answer the question:
**“Can I meaningfully attempt this?”**

---

## Skills Are Contextual

Skills are not universal buttons.

Their effectiveness depends on:
- Situation
- Preparation
- Strain
- Environment
- Tools
- Alignment and resonance

A skilled character still fails in the wrong context.
An unskilled character may succeed with the right leverage.

---

## Progression Is Not Linear

Characters do not improve in straight lines.

Growth occurs through:
- Repetition
- Deliberate training
- Failure and recovery
- Narrative milestones
- Long-term projects
- Transformative events

Some gains are slow.
Some are sudden.
Some are costly.

---

## Breadth and Depth (Revisited)

Progression balances two forces:

- **Breadth** expands what you can attempt  
- **Depth** improves how reliably and efficiently you perform  

This tension is intentional.

A broadly capable character adapts.
A deeply specialised character dominates their domain.

Both are valid.
Neither is free.

---

## Skills, Abilities, and Chakras

Skills do not exist in isolation.

They are shaped by:
- Ability scores (capacity)
- Chakras and mana (alignment and cost)
- Class expression (developmental focus)
- Era context (tools, culture, constraints)

A skill used in alignment costs less and performs more reliably.
Misalignment increases strain and risk.

---

## Training and Use

Skills improve through use — but not all use is equal.

Improvement is driven by:
- Meaningful application
- Acting under pressure
- Deliberate practice
- Instruction or mentorship
- Reflection during downtime

Grinding without intent yields diminishing returns.

---

## Mastery Changes the Shape of Risk

As mastery increases:
- Failure becomes rarer — but more consequential
- Costs reduce — but stakes rise
- Authority increases — but expectations follow

High mastery invites attention.

---

## Progression and Identity

How you grow shapes who you become.

Repeated choices:
- Reinforce identity
- Open new paths
- Close others
- Create narrative gravity

Progression is not just mechanical.
It is autobiographical.

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “What should I optimise?”

Ask instead:
- “What do I keep doing?”
- “What am I willing to train?”
- “What am I becoming known for?”

Skills tell the story of how you survive.

Progression tells the story of who you become.
