# Chakra Mastery and Mana Costs

In Future Sky, power is not free.

Every action draws from somewhere — physical effort, mental focus, emotional stability, or energetic alignment. Chakra mastery determines how efficiently a character can draw on these sources without distortion.

This chapter explains how chakras interact with skills, how mana is spent, and why alignment matters more than raw output.

---

## Chakras as Channels

Chakras are not abilities themselves.

They are channels through which effort, intention, and energy flow.

Each chakra governs:
- A mode of perception or engagement
- A type of energetic cost
- A pattern of distortion when misused
- A path of mastery through integration

Chakras do not grant power.
They shape how power moves.

---

## Mana as Energetic Cost

Mana represents energetic expenditure.

It reflects:
- Focus
- Vitality
- Emotional charge
- Resonant coherence

Mana is spent whenever actions exceed ordinary exertion or alignment.

---

## Chakra Alignment

Actions aligned with a character’s developed chakras:
- Cost less mana
- Generate less strain
- Resolve more cleanly
- Carry fewer side effects

Misaligned actions:
- Cost more mana
- Accumulate strain rapidly
- Risk distortion or backlash
- Produce unstable outcomes

Alignment rewards coherence, not restriction.

---

## Chakra Mastery Levels

Chakra mastery develops gradually.

As mastery increases:
- Mana costs decrease for aligned actions
- Recovery becomes more efficient
- Distortion effects lessen
- Subtle control improves
- Narrative authority expands

Mastery does not eliminate cost.
It optimises flow.

---

## Chakra Strain and Distortion

Overuse or misuse of chakra-aligned energy causes distortion.

Distortion may manifest as:
- Emotional volatility
- Physical symptoms
- Cognitive interference
- Unstable effects
- Narrative consequences

Ignoring distortion invites collapse.

---

## Cross-Chakra Use

Characters may draw from chakras outside their primary alignment.

This allows:
- Flexibility
- Emergency response
- Experimental growth

But cross-chakra use:
- Increases mana cost
- Accelerates strain
- Risks misalignment effects

Versatility has a price.

---

## Chakra and Skill Interaction

Skills are filtered through chakras.

The same skill may:
- Cost different amounts of mana
- Produce different effects
- Carry different risks
- Feel different to perform

Depending on chakra engagement.

Skill mastery reduces mechanical friction.
Chakra mastery reduces energetic friction.

---

## Chakra Growth Through Use

Chakras develop through:
- Repeated aligned action
- Conscious integration
- Reflection and rest
- Navigating strain without collapse
- Resolving distortion

Chakra growth is experiential, not transactional.

---

## Mana Recovery

Mana recovers through:
- Rest and downtime
- Aligned activity
- Safe abodes
- Ritual or reflection
- Environmental harmony

Recovery is faster when coherence is restored.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- Power has texture
- Overreach carries cost
- Alignment feels meaningful
- Chakra work integrates with play
- No character operates without consequence

Energy remembers how it is used.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “How much mana do I have?”

Ask instead:
- “Where am I drawing from?”
- “What am I misaligned with?”
- “What will this cost me later?”

Power flows best when it is respected.
