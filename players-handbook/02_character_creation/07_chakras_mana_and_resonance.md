# Chakras, Mana, and Resonance

In Future Sky, power is not separate from the self.

Every action draws on internal resources — attention, intention, balance, and capacity. These resources are modelled through **chakras**, **mana**, and **resonance**.

This system does not represent belief or spirituality.
It represents **where effort comes from**, and what it costs to sustain.

---

## Chakras as Functional Centres

Chakras in Future Sky are not metaphysical abstractions.

They are **functional centres of action** — ways in which a character engages the world through body, emotion, perception, and intent. Each chakra governs a mode of interaction that can be developed, strained, or depleted.

All characters have access to all chakras.
No character uses them equally.

---

## The Five Elemental Chakras

Future Sky tracks five primary chakras, each associated with an elemental mana pool.

### Muladhara — Earth (Smell)

Grounding, survival, and physical stability.

Earth mana supports endurance, resistance, and bodily awareness. Characters drawing heavily on Earth excel at holding ground, surviving hardship, and maintaining presence under pressure.

Imbalance manifests as rigidity, fear, or exhaustion.

---

### Svadhisthana — Water (Taste)

Flow, movement, and adaptability.

Water mana governs momentum, relationship, and change. It supports traversal, emotional intelligence, and responsiveness.

Imbalance manifests as instability, indulgence, or loss of direction.

---

### Manipura — Fire (Vision)

Will, agency, and transformation.

Fire mana fuels decisive action, leadership, and the power to alter conditions. It enables initiative and forceful intervention.

Imbalance manifests as burnout, domination, or reckless escalation.

---

### Anahata — Air (Touch)

Connection, trust, and exchange.

Air mana governs relationships, coordination, and mutual influence. It supports teamwork, healing, and mediation.

Imbalance manifests as dependency, isolation, or emotional overload.

---

### Vishuddha — Ether (Hearing)

Expression, signal, and translation.

Ether mana enables communication, interpretation, and interface — between people, systems, symbols, and intelligences.

Imbalance manifests as distortion, miscommunication, or loss of voice.

---

## Spirit Power — Ajna and Sahasrara

Beyond the elemental chakras lies **Spirit Power**, associated with Ajna and Sahasrara.

Spirit power governs:
- Insight beyond linear perception
- Temporal awareness
- Quantum or rule-breaking effects
- Grace, coherence, and transcendence

Spirit power is potent and costly.

It is not a standard resource and cannot be sustained indefinitely. Drawing on Spirit often bypasses normal constraints — but leaves lasting consequences.

---

## Mana as Exertion, Not Fuel

Mana in Future Sky does not represent spell slots or magical energy reserves.

It represents **exertion**.

Spending mana reflects effort, strain, and commitment. Using aligned mana feels natural and sustainable. Drawing on non-aligned mana is possible, but costly.

Overextension leads not just to depletion, but to imbalance.

---

## Resonance

Resonance describes how aligned a character currently is with their internal state and external context.

High resonance:
- Reduces cost
- Improves clarity
- Enables peak moments

Low resonance:
- Increases strain
- Distorts outcomes
- Introduces unintended consequences

Resonance fluctuates through play. It is influenced by:
- Choices under pressure
- Emotional state
- Environment
- Narrative context

Resonance is not a moral score.
It is a measure of coherence.

---

## Class Alignment and Cost

Each class naturally resonates with certain chakras and elements.

Acting within alignment is efficient.
Acting outside it is possible — but expensive.

This allows:
- Flexible play
- Meaningful trade-offs
- Characters to grow beyond initial limitations

Mastery involves knowing **when** to strain and **when** to rest.

---

## Growth and Imbalance

Chakras develop unevenly.

Some awaken early.
Some lag behind.
Some destabilise under stress.

Imbalance is not failure.
It is information.

Ignoring imbalance leads to collapse.
Responding to it leads to growth.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- All characters use internal resources
- Power carries cost
- Growth reflects lived experience
- Mechanics reinforce narrative identity

You cannot optimise away consequence.
You must engage with it.

---

## A Final Orientation

You do not need to track every chakra perfectly.

Pay attention to:
- Where your character feels strong
- Where they feel strained
- What kinds of actions cost more than expected

The system will respond.

Balance is not achieved by control.
It is achieved through attention.
