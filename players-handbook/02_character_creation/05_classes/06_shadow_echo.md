# Shadow-Echo

The Shadow-Echo acts where attention is thin.

They understand that not all power announces itself. Some power works by *withholding*, by delaying, by letting effects unfold long after the initial action has vanished from sight.

Where others move openly, the Shadow-Echo leaves traces.

---

## Class Fantasy

Shadow-Echoes operate in negative space.

They notice what is missing, what is unguarded, what is assumed to be inert. Their actions are small, precise, and often invisible — but their consequences reverberate.

Across eras, Shadow-Echoes appear as:
- Scouts, infiltrators, and observers
- Messengers who are never noticed
- Saboteurs and quiet protectors
- Operatives who work through reflection rather than force

They are not defined by violence.
They are defined by *timing*.

---

## Role in the World

Shadow-Echoes excel at:
- Operating unseen or unnoticed
- Exploiting blind spots in systems and perception
- Acting without escalating conflict
- Creating delayed or indirect effects

They often become:
- The reason an enemy plan fails quietly
- The unseen guardian of vulnerable spaces
- The first to notice an approaching threat
- The one who leaves before being thanked

They do not seek recognition.
They seek outcome.

---

## Era Expressions

The Shadow-Echo archetype persists across eras, shaped by how power hides itself.

### Jyotzon Era — The Night-Walker

In the ancient future, Shadow-Echoes move through myth and shadow.

They act as watchers, messengers between clans, and guardians of forbidden thresholds. Darkness is not evil — it is shelter, secrecy, and depth.

Their challenge is restraint without isolation.

---

### Omnicron Era — The Ghost Operator

In the collapsing present, Shadow-Echoes work within surveillance and decay.

They exploit blind spots in monitoring systems, slip between collapsing institutions, and act where visibility has become dangerous.

Their challenge is invisibility without erasure.

---

### Manzo Era — The Phase Drifter

In the far frontier, Shadow-Echoes manipulate absence itself.

They move between layers of reality — temporal offsets, probability gaps, digital shadows. Presence becomes optional. Consequence is engineered.

Their challenge is power without detachment.

---

## Resonance and Alignment

Shadow-Echoes naturally resonate with:
- **Ajna** (awareness, perception)
- **Muladhara** (survival, grounding)
- **Ajna / Spirit overlap** (liminality, threshold states)

They are often aligned with:
- **Air** and **Ether** mana
- Subtle, intermittent elemental flows

They can draw on Fire for decisive action, but excess exposure risks over-commitment and detection.

Balance is maintained through restraint.

---

## Core Mechanical Identity

At a high level, Shadow-Echoes:
- Bypass direct confrontation
- Create advantages through positioning and timing
- Manipulate awareness, attention, and presence
- Influence outcomes without attribution

They are strongest when:
- Information asymmetry exists
- Observation matters
- Quiet intervention prevents escalation

They struggle when:
- Forced into prolonged visibility
- Required to act without preparation
- Denied access to movement or concealment

---

## Signature Moment

At peak resonance, a Shadow-Echo executes **perfect omission**.

They remove a single, critical element — a message, a guard, a signal, a moment — and the entire structure collapses on its own.

No one sees them act.
Everyone feels the result.

---

## Advancement Themes

Shadow-Echoes grow by:
- Refining patience and discipline
- Expanding the scale of influence without increasing visibility
- Learning when *not* to act
- Maintaining identity while operating unseen

Excess caution leads to stagnation.
Excess action leads to exposure.

Mastery lies in selective presence.

---

## Playing a Shadow-Echo

Choose this class if you enjoy:
- Stealth without spectacle
- Tactical patience
- Indirect problem-solving
- Letting consequences unfold

A Shadow-Echo does not dominate the scene.

They decide which scenes ever occur.
