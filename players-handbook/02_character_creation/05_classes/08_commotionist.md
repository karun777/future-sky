# Commotionist

The Commotionist creates movement where systems have settled.

They understand that stagnation is not stability — it is stored energy waiting to rupture. Rather than suppress disruption, the Commotionist **guides it**, turning chaos into momentum and fracture into possibility.

They are not reckless.
They are catalytic.

---

## Class Fantasy

Commotionists live at the edge of order.

They sense when systems have become too rigid, when rules no longer serve life, when predictability has become a liability. Their power lies in introducing *just enough instability* to force adaptation.

Across eras, Commotionists appear as:
- Tricksters, artists, and provocateurs
- Culture-jammers and agitators
- Wild innovators and dangerous friends
- Those who laugh when the world takes itself too seriously

They are not here to burn everything down.
They are here to make sure something new can grow.

---

## Role in the World

Commotionists excel at:
- Disrupting entrenched systems
- Shifting emotional and narrative momentum
- Breaking deadlocks and stalemates
- Forcing attention onto ignored truths

They often become:
- The unexpected turning point
- The reason a plan survives first contact with reality
- The spark that ignites collective action
- The one everyone blames — and secretly thanks

They act where politeness has failed.

---

## Era Expressions

The Commotionist archetype persists across eras, shaped by how tightly order grips the world.

### Jyotzon Era — The Sacred Trickster

In the ancient future, Commotionists serve a ritual role.

They break taboos to reveal their limits, disrupt ceremony to test its strength, and ensure that tradition remains alive rather than ossified.

Their challenge is irreverence without sacrilege.

---

### Omnicron Era — The Culture Jammer

In the collapsing present, Commotionists weaponise expression.

They hack narratives, disrupt propaganda, and turn spectacle into critique. Art becomes resistance. Noise becomes signal.

Their challenge is visibility without burnout.

---

### Manzo Era — The Chaos Engineer

In the far frontier, Commotionists manipulate complexity itself.

They seed instability into over-optimised systems, introduce randomness into closed loops, and prevent monocultures of thought, code, or governance.

Their challenge is freedom without collapse.

---

## Resonance and Alignment

Commotionists naturally resonate with:
- **Manipura** (will, transformation)
- **Svadhisthana** (flow, creativity)
- **Vishuddha** (expression, disruption)

They are often aligned with:
- **Fire** and **Air** mana
- Volatile, expressive elemental flows

They can draw on Spirit power, but excess destabilisation risks losing coherence entirely.

Chaos still needs a container.

---

## Core Mechanical Identity

At a high level, Commotionists:
- Break patterns and expectations
- Introduce randomness or volatility
- Shift emotional or narrative states
- Force systems to adapt or fail

They are strongest when:
- Situations are stuck
- Systems are over-optimised
- Conformity suppresses truth

They struggle when:
- Stability is required for survival
- Subtlety is necessary
- Consequences of disruption are ignored

---

## Signature Moment

At peak resonance, a Commotionist triggers **creative rupture**.

A single disruptive act releases trapped energy across a system. What emerges is unpredictable — but alive. New paths appear where none existed moments before.

This moment is dangerous.
It is also necessary.

---

## Advancement Themes

Commotionists grow by:
- Learning restraint without losing edge
- Understanding when disruption heals — and when it harms
- Developing containers for chaos
- Taking responsibility for what follows the spark

Unbounded disruption leads to collapse.
Suppressed disruption leads to rot.

Mastery lies in *timed chaos*.

---

## Playing a Commotionist

Choose this class if you enjoy:
- Creative problem-solving
- Shaking things loose
- Emotional and narrative impact
- Acting when others hesitate

A Commotionist does not bring order.

They make change unavoidable.
