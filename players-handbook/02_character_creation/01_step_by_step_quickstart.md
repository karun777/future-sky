# Step-by-Step Quickstart

This section walks you through creating a playable Future Sky character as quickly and clearly as possible.

You do not need to understand every system before you begin.
You do not need to make perfect choices.
You do not need to optimise.

You are choosing a **starting posture toward the world**.
Everything else will evolve.

---

## Step 1: Choose a Core Identity

Before selecting numbers or abilities, decide who your character is at a fundamental level.

Consider:
- What kind of person are they?
- What do they pay attention to?
- What are they trying to protect, escape, or understand?

This does not need to be detailed.
A sentence or two is enough.

You will refine this identity through play.

---

## Step 2: Assign Ability Scores

Next, assign your character’s ability scores.

These represent broad competencies such as physical capability, perception, intellect, resilience, and presence. If you are familiar with d20 systems, this process will feel familiar.

Choose a method appropriate to your group or platform:
- Standard array
- Point buy
- Rolled scores

Do not worry about optimisation yet.
Choose scores that support the character you imagine playing.

---

## Step 3: Select a Background or Origin

Your background represents where your character comes from and how they learned to survive.

Backgrounds provide:
- Narrative context
- Skill proficiencies
- Starting resources
- Access to certain social or environmental situations

Your background helps explain **why** your character can do what they can do.

---

## Step 4: Choose a Class Archetype

Select a class that best reflects how your character engages with the world.

Classes in Future Sky are archetypal. They describe enduring patterns of behaviour and problem-solving rather than narrow job roles.

When choosing a class, focus on:
- The fantasy of the archetype
- How it approaches conflict and uncertainty
- How it might evolve across different eras

Do not worry yet about exact abilities or numbers.
Those will come later.

---

## Step 5: Consider Era Expression

Your character exists within a particular era.

The same class will express itself differently depending on whether the world is:
- Ancient and mythic
- Fragmented and collapsing
- Frontier, hybrid, and far-future

You do not need to lock this in perfectly.
Era expression provides **context**, not restriction.

---

## Step 6: Note Resonance and Tendencies

Each character has internal tendencies shaped by:
- Chakra alignment
- Elemental flow
- Timing and circumstance

At character creation, this is mostly descriptive.

You might note:
- Which elements feel natural
- Where tension or imbalance exists
- Which aspects of self feel undeveloped

These qualities will gain mechanical meaning over time.

---

## Step 7: Choose Starting Equipment and Resources

Based on your class and background, choose your starting equipment.

This represents what your character has access to, not everything they might need.

Equipment in Future Sky often carries narrative weight.
An item may matter because of where it came from, not just what it does.

---

## Step 8: Begin in the World

Once these steps are complete, your character is ready to enter the world.

You do not need a finished backstory.
You do not need to know your long-term arc.
You do not need to anticipate every outcome.

The first session is about orientation.

Pay attention.
Make small choices.
Notice how the world responds.

---

## A Final Reminder

Character creation in Future Sky is not a test.

It is an invitation.

If something feels unclear, it will become clearer through play.
If a choice feels imperfect, the world will give you opportunities to respond.

Begin where you are.

The system will meet you there.
