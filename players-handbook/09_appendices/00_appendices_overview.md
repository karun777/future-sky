# Appendices

The appendices exist to support play.

They provide reference, clarification, examples, and orientation without interrupting the flow of the main text. Nothing in this section introduces new rules. Everything here exists to make the rest of the book easier to use, remember, and return to.

This section is designed to be dipped into as needed.

---

## What the Appendices Are For

The appendices serve four primary purposes:

- **Reference** — Clear definitions and summaries
- **Accessibility** — Quick access during play
- **Illustration** — Concrete examples of systems in motion
- **Orientation** — A shared language for the table

They are tools, not requirements.

---

## Reading Order

The appendices do not need to be read sequentially.

Use them:
- Before play, to orient
- During play, to clarify
- After play, to reflect
- Between sessions, to prepare

They are designed to be non-linear.

---

## What You’ll Find Here

This section includes:

- A **Glossary** of core terms and concepts  
- A **Quick Reference** for common procedures  
- A **Character Sheet Template**  
- **Sample Characters** to illustrate archetypes  
- **Sample Encounters** showing systems in use  
- A **Design Philosophy One-Pager** explaining intent  

Each appendix stands alone.

---

## Authority and Flexibility

The appendices clarify intent but do not override table judgement.

If something here conflicts with lived play:
- Discuss it
- Adjust it
- Record what changed

Future Sky is designed to adapt.

---

## For Facilitators and Players

Some appendices may feel more facilitator-facing.
Others are player-oriented.

All are player-readable.

Transparency is preferred over mystery at this layer.

---

## Updating and Iteration

The appendices are expected to evolve.

They are the most likely place for:
- Clarifications
- Examples
- Revisions
- Optional additions

Treat them as living documents.

---

## Why This Matters

This section ensures that:
- The game remains playable under pressure
- New players can onboard quickly
- Experienced tables can stay aligned
- The system remains legible over time

Good reference reduces friction.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “Do I need to memorise this?”

Ask instead:
- “What would help us right now?”
- “What would make this clearer?”
- “What would we look up again later?”

The appendices are here when you need them.
