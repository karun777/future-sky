# Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used throughout *Future Sky*.  
Terms are written for clarity, not exhaustiveness. Where a term carries layered meaning, the definition reflects how it functions in play.

---

## Action
A declared intent to affect the world. Actions may be physical, social, cognitive, or symbolic, and always carry potential consequence.

## Ambient Effects
Low-intensity influences that occur over time, shaping mood, strain, and perception without demanding immediate response.

## Ability Scores
Core measures of a character’s physical, mental, and social capacity. In Future Sky, abilities can grow through use and training.

## Abode
A personal or shared place of rest, storage, reflection, and recovery. Abodes support downtime actions and long-term projects.

## Branching (Era Branching)
The process by which skills and capabilities express differently across eras without fragmenting character identity.

## Chakra
An energetic channel governing how effort, perception, and intent flow. Chakras shape mana cost, strain, and distortion.

## Chakra Mastery
The degree to which a character can draw on a chakra efficiently and coherently, reducing cost and risk.

## Comet
A meta-system representing inevitable approach and delayed consequence. Comets apply world-scale pressure and drive convergence.

## Consequence
Any lasting effect produced by action or inaction. Consequence may be immediate, delayed, personal, social, or systemic.

## Cube
A personal meta-artefact bound to each character. The Cube functions as a vault, record, map, and threshold, quietly bending rules.

## Distortion
The side effects of misaligned or excessive energy use. Distortion manifests emotionally, physically, narratively, or systemically.

## Downtime
A phase of play focused on recovery, training, projects, and reflection, where time is abstracted and pressure is reduced.

## Event
A world response to accumulated conditions. Events may be ambient, triggered, or escalated, and do not require player presence.

## Faction
An organised group with shared interests, internal dynamics, and memory. Factions act independently and respond to player action.

## Hard Time
A mode of play where timing, order, and urgency matter. Hard time uses turns or structured windows and escalates consequence.

## IRL Node
An optional real-world anchor linked to the game symbolically or digitally. IRL nodes are always opt-in and consent-based.

## Mana
A measure of energetic expenditure representing focus, vitality, and resonance. Mana is spent when actions exceed ordinary exertion.

## Memory Node
A location, object, or moment that retains narrative and energetic residue across time and timelines.

## Milestone
A significant narrative or experiential turning point that unlocks progression, integration, or new capabilities.

## Pressure Window
A bounded period of heightened consequence where options narrow and timing matters.

## Project
A structured long-term effort pursued during downtime, such as research, construction, healing, or influence-building.

## Reputation
The accumulated signal of how individuals or groups perceive a character based on actions and outcomes.

## Soft Time
The default mode of play where timing is abstracted and actions resolve narratively rather than in strict order.

## Strain
Accumulated stress from overexertion, misalignment, or pressure. Excess strain leads to distortion or failure.

## Talent
A learned pattern of action representing a breakthrough or refined technique. Talents shape how characters act.

## Thread
A persistent narrative tension or unresolved force that generates events and encounters over time.

## Timeline
A dominant pattern of cause and effect within the shared world. Multiple timelines may coexist and interfere.

## World Mood
The prevailing emotional and environmental tone of an area or period, influenced by events and pressure.

---

## Using This Glossary

If a term feels unclear during play:
- Pause briefly
- Check this glossary
- Agree on interpretation
- Continue

Clarity supports flow.
