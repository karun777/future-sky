# Sample Characters

The following sample characters illustrate how Future Sky characters can express familiar archetypes while remaining shaped by era, context, and consequence.

These are not optimised builds.  
They are *coherent lives in motion*.

Use them as inspiration, pre-generated characters, or teaching tools.

---

## Isha Calder  
**Class:** Rebel-Architect  
**Era of Expression:** Omnicron  
**Background:** Community Organiser / Systems Breaker  

Isha grew up watching infrastructure fail slowly — power grids, food networks, trust. She learned early that rebellion without structure burns out, and structure without rebellion calcifies.

She plans in public and conspires in private.

**Strengths**
- Strategic thinking under pressure  
- Social leverage and reputation play  
- Turning scarcity into coordination  

**Challenges**
- Carries strain from constant responsibility  
- Struggles to rest without guilt  

**Cube Note:**  
Isha’s Cube contains layered maps of abandoned systems — some still humming.

---

## Korrin of the Red Path  
**Class:** Primal-Rider  
**Era of Expression:** Jyotzon  
**Background:** Clan Scout / Beast-Bound  

Korrin rides with the land rather than over it. Their bond-beast is not owned, only agreed with. When the ground trembles, Korrin listens.

Violence is not their preference — but neither is hesitation.

**Strengths**
- Mobility and environmental mastery  
- Intuitive perception of threat  
- Physical resilience  

**Challenges**
- Difficulty navigating rigid social systems  
- Distrust of abstraction and bureaucracy  

**Cube Note:**  
Korrin’s Cube smells faintly of ash and rain, and resists attempts to categorise its contents.

---

## Emet Vale  
**Class:** Mystic-Kin  
**Era of Expression:** Manzo  
**Background:** Archivist / Signal Listener  

Emet hears patterns others dismiss as noise. Dreams arrive structured. Memories echo forward. They are calm, not because they understand everything — but because they accept not understanding.

Others find Emet reassuring. Some find them unsettling.

**Strengths**
- Insight into hidden connections  
- Emotional steadiness under stress  
- Chakra efficiency  

**Challenges**
- Overextension through empathy  
- Difficulty acting decisively in conflict  

**Cube Note:**  
Emet’s Cube occasionally records events before they occur.

---

## Nyx Calderon  
**Class:** Shadow-Echo  
**Era of Expression:** Omnicron  
**Background:** Courier / Ghost in the System  

Nyx moves through places designed to exclude them. Doors open. Locks fail. Names change. Nyx leaves little behind — except rumours.

They do not see themselves as a hero. Just necessary.

**Strengths**
- Stealth and evasion  
- Information acquisition  
- Operating unseen  

**Challenges**
- Fragile trust relationships  
- Accumulated distortion from prolonged concealment  

**Cube Note:**  
Nyx’s Cube has been wiped clean more than once — unsuccessfully.

---

## Talaris Quell  
**Class:** Astro-Cartographer  
**Era of Expression:** Manzo  
**Background:** Navigator / Pattern Analyst  

Talaris charts not where things are, but where they *will be*. Orbits, migrations, collapses — all leave traces if you know how to read them.

They are patient, precise, and quietly alarmed most of the time.

**Strengths**
- Long-term planning  
- Predictive insight  
- Navigational mastery  

**Challenges**
- Paralysis when patterns diverge  
- Difficulty improvising emotionally  

**Cube Note:**  
Talaris’s Cube contains star maps that do not correspond to known skies.

---

## Using Sample Characters

You may:
- Use these characters as-is
- Modify details freely
- Swap eras or backgrounds
- Strip them down to mechanics only

They exist to show how:
- Class does not dictate personality
- Era reshapes expression
- The Cube adapts to the bearer
- Growth emerges through play

---

## Facilitator Tip

When introducing new players:
- Hand out a sample character
- Start in motion
- Explain systems as they arise

Understanding follows action.
