# Pressure Windows and World Events

Not all moments are equal.

Sometimes the world tightens around a narrow window of opportunity — a period where choices must be made, actions must be taken, and hesitation carries immediate cost. These are pressure windows. When pressure windows expand beyond a local context, they become world events.

This chapter explains how urgency crystallises, how large-scale events unfold, and why timing matters.

---

## What Is a Pressure Window?

A pressure window is a bounded period of heightened consequence.

During a pressure window:
- Options narrow
- Costs escalate
- Outcomes harden quickly
- Delay resolves automatically
- The world is actively responding

Pressure windows are not surprises.
They are the result of accumulated tension.

---

## Signals Before the Window

Pressure windows are usually preceded by signals.

These may include:
- Escalating ambient effects
- Repeated warnings
- Environmental instability
- Shifts in faction behaviour
- Increased surveillance or hostility

Ignoring signals compresses the window later.

---

## Entering a Pressure Window

A pressure window may be triggered by:
- Player action
- Player inaction
- External events
- Thresholds being crossed
- Timers reaching zero

Once open, the window remains until resolved or collapsed.

---

## Playing Inside the Window

Inside a pressure window:
- Hard time often applies
- Actions resolve quickly
- Strain accumulates faster
- Mistakes compound
- Success requires commitment

Pressure reveals priorities.

---

## Resolution and Aftermath

When a pressure window closes:
- The world stabilises into a new state
- Consequences surface
- Access may change
- Relationships shift
- New threads may emerge

Closure does not mean restoration.

---

## World Events

World events are pressure windows at scale.

They may affect:
- Regions
- Factions
- Economies
- Environments
- Timelines

World events do not pause for individual characters.

---

## Participation and Absence

Players may:
- Engage directly
- Influence events indirectly
- Arrive too late
- Be affected without being present

Absence is not immunity.

---

## Parallel Pressure

Multiple pressure windows may exist simultaneously.

Choosing where to act means:
- Accepting loss elsewhere
- Delegating responsibility
- Living with partial outcomes

You cannot be everywhere.

---

## Temporal Weight

Some pressure windows echo across time.

Their outcomes may:
- Alter future eras
- Leave scars in memory
- Become historical reference points
- Shape world mythology

History is written under pressure.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- Urgency feels earned
- Large events emerge logically
- Player choice shapes history
- The world remains dynamic
- Time carries narrative weight

Moments matter because they end.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “How do we stop this?”

Ask instead:
- “What can we still change?”
- “What must we accept?”
- “What happens if we do nothing?”

The window is already closing.
