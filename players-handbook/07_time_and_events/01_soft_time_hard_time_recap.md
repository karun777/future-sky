# Soft Time and Hard Time

Time in Future Sky shifts modes.

Sometimes it flows loosely, allowing exploration, reflection, and asynchronous play. Other times it tightens, demanding precision, urgency, and decisive action. These two modes — soft time and hard time — allow the game to breathe without losing tension.

This chapter clarifies when each mode applies and how players can recognise the shift.

---

## Soft Time

Soft time is the default state of play.

In soft time:
- Actions are descriptive and flexible
- Exact timing is abstracted
- Players act asynchronously
- Scenes unfold at narrative pace
- Focus is on intention and outcome

Soft time supports:
- Exploration
- Social interaction
- Planning and preparation
- Travel
- Downtime and projects

Soft time allows the world to be lived in.

---

## What Soft Time Does Not Mean

Soft time does not mean:
- No consequences
- No urgency
- No world movement
- Unlimited action

It means timing is contextual rather than exact.

---

## Hard Time

Hard time activates when precision matters.

In hard time:
- Actions occur in structured turns or windows
- Order and timing affect outcomes
- Delays carry risk
- Pressure escalates rapidly
- Missed actions resolve automatically

Hard time is used for:
- Combat
- Chases
- Emergencies
- Time-critical operations
- High-stakes negotiations

Hard time sharpens the moment.

---

## Transitioning Between Modes

Transitions are triggered by:
- Rising threat
- Imminent consequence
- Environmental escalation
- Direct opposition
- Explicit player choice

The shift should be clear and felt.

---

## Player Awareness

Players are always informed when hard time begins.

Clarity includes:
- What is at stake
- What is being tracked
- What happens if you do nothing
- How long pressure will last

Uncertainty comes from the world, not the rules.

---

## Acting in Hard Time

During hard time:
- Actions are limited
- Intent must be clear
- Hesitation resolves into default behaviour
- Strain accumulates faster
- Mistakes are costly

Hard time reveals habits.

---

## Returning to Soft Time

When pressure resolves, play returns to soft time.

This transition allows:
- Reflection
- Recovery
- Consequence to surface
- Narrative integration

The aftermath matters.

---

## Mixed-Time Play

Some situations blend modes.

For example:
- Soft time exploration interrupted by hard time danger
- Hard time conflict followed by soft time negotiation
- Ongoing pressure within a flexible window

The mode reflects the situation, not the genre.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- Urgency feels earned
- Async play remains viable
- Tabletop and digital play align
- Tension is readable
- Time serves story, not bookkeeping

You do not track time constantly.

Time tracks you.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “Are we in turns yet?”

Ask instead:
- “What happens if we wait?”
- “What happens if we rush?”
- “What does the world demand right now?”

The mode has already shifted.
