# Events, Encounters, and Threads

In Future Sky, stories are not pre-written paths.

They are threads — tensions, signals, and unresolved conditions that respond to player action and world state. Events and encounters emerge from these threads rather than interrupting play at random.

This chapter explains how events are generated, how encounters function, and how narrative threads persist over time.

---

## Events as World Responses

An event is the world responding to conditions.

Events may be triggered by:
- Player action
- Player inaction
- Environmental pressure
- Faction agendas
- Accumulated tension
- Temporal instability

Events do not exist in isolation.
They arise because something has been left unresolved.

---

## Encounters as Focused Moments

Encounters are moments where attention narrows.

They may involve:
- Conflict
- Revelation
- Choice
- Risk
- Opportunity
- Loss

An encounter brings specific elements into sharp focus without halting the broader world.

---

## Threads as Persistent Tension

Threads represent ongoing narrative forces.

A thread may be:
- A brewing conflict
- A hidden threat
- An unanswered question
- A developing relationship
- A systemic instability

Threads persist until resolved, transformed, or collapsed.

---

## Thread Visibility

Not all threads are visible.

Threads may be:
- Obvious and public
- Partially known
- Misunderstood
- Entirely hidden

Discovery often reveals the existence of a thread before its nature.

---

## Thread Escalation

Threads escalate over time.

Escalation may include:
- Increasing frequency of events
- Rising stakes
- Narrowing options
- External pressure
- Forced confrontation

Ignoring a thread rarely neutralises it.

---

## Player Interaction with Threads

Players influence threads through:
- Direct action
- Indirect consequence
- Alliances formed or broken
- Information revealed or concealed
- Timing choices

Resolution is not always clean.

---

## Branching Outcomes

Threads may:
- Resolve peacefully
- Explode into conflict
- Mutate into something new
- Collapse and resurface later
- Leave scars on the world

Outcomes depend on context, not templates.

---

## Ambient Events

Some events occur without direct provocation.

Ambient events:
- Reinforce world mood
- Signal shifting conditions
- Provide subtle information
- Create texture and continuity

They remind players that the world is moving.

---

## Encounter Density

Not every moment needs an encounter.

Space between events allows:
- Reflection
- Planning
- Character interaction
- Consequence to surface

Pacing matters.

---

## Why This Matters

This approach ensures that:
- Stories feel emergent
- Player choice drives narrative
- The world remains coherent
- Repetition is avoided
- Events feel earned

You are not following a plot.

You are navigating a field of forces.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “What is supposed to happen next?”

Ask instead:
- “What is unresolved?”
- “What is tightening?”
- “What have we disturbed?”

Threads pull whether you notice them or not.
