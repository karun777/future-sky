# World Interaction

The world of Future Sky is not a backdrop.

It responds, resists, remembers, and reshapes itself around player action. Exploration, social engagement, and environmental interaction are core modes of play — not filler between combat scenes.

This section explains how characters move through the world, uncover its layers, influence its inhabitants, and contend with its hazards.

---

## The World as a Living System

Locations in Future Sky are not static.

They have:
- Histories
- Power dynamics
- Environmental pressures
- Cultural norms
- Hidden layers and thresholds

What you do in one place may echo elsewhere.

---

## Exploration as Inquiry

Exploration is not just movement.

It includes:
- Investigating environments
- Reading spatial cues
- Testing boundaries
- Discovering hidden systems
- Interpreting what the world is telling you

The world communicates through detail.

---

## Social Interaction as Play

Social engagement is mechanically meaningful.

Reputation, trust, fear, and obligation shape outcomes just as much as force. Conversations can:
- Open paths
- Close doors
- Shift alliances
- Trigger long-term consequences
- Alter how the world treats you

Words are actions.

---

## Tools, Objects, and Improvisation

Items are not just bonuses.

They are:
- Tools
- Leverage
- Narrative anchors
- Environmental modifiers

Improvisation is encouraged.
Creative use often matters more than raw stats.

---

## Environments as Pressure

The world applies pressure even when no enemy is present.

Hazards may include:
- Weather
- Terrain
- Scarcity
- Surveillance
- Social tension
- Temporal instability

Survival is not always about combat.

---

## Knowledge as Currency

Information has value.

Knowing:
- Where to go
- Who to talk to
- What not to touch
- When to act
- What has already changed

Can be more powerful than equipment.

---

## Player Agency and World Response

The world tracks what you do.

Repeated behaviour may:
- Alter reputation
- Change access
- Shift environmental states
- Unlock or seal content
- Create emergent storylines

The world does not reset between scenes.

---

## Scale of Interaction

World interaction operates at multiple scales:
- Immediate (room or zone)
- Local (neighbourhood or faction)
- Regional (travel routes or regions)
- Temporal (effects across time and eras)

Small actions can ripple outward.

---

## Why This Matters

This approach ensures that:
- Exploration is meaningful
- Social play carries weight
- The environment participates in play
- Creativity is rewarded
- The world feels alive

You are not passing through the world.

You are changing it.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “What can I roll here?”

Ask instead:
- “What is happening?”
- “What does this place want?”
- “What has already been disturbed?”

The world is always speaking.

You just have to listen.
