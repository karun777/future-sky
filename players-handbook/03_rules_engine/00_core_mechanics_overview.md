# Core Mechanics of Play — Overview

Future Sky is a game about agency under pressure.

You will make choices with incomplete information. You will act inside systems that do not pause for you. You will succeed, fail, adapt, and carry the consequences forward.

The rules exist to support that experience — not to replace it.

This section explains how play works moment to moment: how you act, how time behaves, how outcomes resolve, how failure matters, and how characters change over long arcs.

---

## What This Game Feels Like

Future Sky supports two truths at once:

1. **Most of the time, you can play asynchronously.**  
   You can act when you can, respond later, and still feel part of a shared world.

2. **Sometimes, time suddenly matters.**  
   In combat, emergencies, social flashpoints, and world events, delay becomes a choice with consequences.

This is not a contradiction. It is the rhythm of the world.

---

## The Core Loop

Most play follows a simple loop:

1. **Perceive**  
   You look, listen, inspect, ask questions, read a room, notice patterns.

2. **Declare intent**  
   You state what you are trying to do — not just the button you press.

3. **Act**  
   The action resolves through ability, skill, tools, resonance, and context.

4. **Pay cost**  
   Even clean success can cost time, attention, strain, resources, or social positioning.

5. **Carry consequence**  
   The world changes. You change. Future actions are shaped by what happened.

If you understand this loop, you understand the system.

---

## Dice and Determinism

Future Sky uses d20-style resolution because it is legible.

But dice are not the centre of the experience.

A roll is used when:
- The outcome is uncertain
- The consequences matter
- The moment is meaningfully contested by the world

Many actions do not require rolls.
Some actions are resolved by context alone.
Sometimes the most important choice is whether to act at all.

---

## Context Is a Rule

In Future Sky, “context” is not flavour. It is a mechanical truth.

The same action can resolve differently depending on:
- Environment
- Preparation and tools
- Emotional state
- Resonance and mana alignment
- Social positioning
- Timing and urgency

This makes the world feel alive — and prevents optimisation from replacing judgement.

---

## Growth Without Fragmentation

Characters develop over time.

They change through:
- Skill mastery
- Chakra alignment and imbalance
- Training and recovery
- Repeated choices under pressure

This system is built for long play.

You are not assembling a build.
You are becoming someone.

---

## Combat Is Not the Whole Game

Combat is one expression of conflict.

It matters. It carries risk. It can be intense.

But Future Sky is not a combat simulator. Many conflicts resolve through:
- Negotiation
- Withdrawal
- Preparation
- Misdirection
- Creative disruption
- Systems thinking

Combat is respected, not centralised.

---

## Why These Rules Exist

The rules are designed to ensure:
- Actions matter
- Costs are real
- Failure teaches rather than punishes
- The world remains coherent across eras
- Mystery can exist without confusion

You should feel free to improvise within the system.

The rules are a scaffold for meaning.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask, “What is the optimal move?”

Ask:
- “What does my character try?”
- “What does it cost?”
- “What changes if I succeed or fail?”
- “What pattern am I reinforcing?”

The world will answer.
