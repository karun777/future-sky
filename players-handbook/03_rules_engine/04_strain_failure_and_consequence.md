# Strain, Failure, and Consequence

In Future Sky, cost accumulates.

Not all at once.
Not always visibly.
But inevitably.

Strain, failure, and consequence are how the world remembers what you do.

---

## Strain as Accumulated Pressure

Strain represents the toll of sustained effort, misalignment, and overreach.

It may reflect:
- Physical exhaustion
- Emotional overload
- Cognitive fatigue
- Spiritual or existential imbalance
- Systemic stress from repeated demand

Strain is not damage.
It is warning.

---

## Sources of Strain

Strain accumulates through:
- Repeated exertion without recovery
- Acting outside natural alignment
- Operating under sustained pressure
- Absorbing unresolved consequences
- Pushing through uncertainty without preparation

You may not notice strain immediately.
That is part of the danger.

---

## What Strain Does

As strain increases, it may:
- Increase action cost
- Reduce clarity or precision
- Narrow available options
- Introduce distortion or delay
- Trigger narrative complications

High strain makes even simple actions harder.
Low strain allows resilience.

---

## Failure Is Not a Dead End

Failure in Future Sky is not a stop.
It is a redirection.

A failed action may:
- Reveal hidden constraints
- Escalate tension
- Shift danger elsewhere
- Force exposure or loss
- Introduce new choices

Nothing happens “for nothing”.

---

## Soft Failure and Hard Failure

Not all failures are equal.

- **Soft failure** changes the situation without catastrophic loss.
- **Hard failure** introduces irreversible consequence.

The difference is often determined by:
- Pressure level
- Accumulated strain
- Stakes of the moment
- Willingness to push further

Repeated soft failure often leads to hard failure.
Ignoring warning signs accelerates collapse.

---

## Compounding Consequence

Consequences stack.

An unresolved outcome may:
- Reappear later
- Amplify under stress
- Combine with other failures
- Create unexpected vulnerability

The world does not reset between scenes.
It remembers patterns.

---

## Success Can Still Cost

Even clean success may generate consequence.

Examples include:
- Drawing attention
- Creating obligation
- Burning bridges
- Increasing expectation
- Setting precedents you must now uphold

Power is rarely invisible.

---

## Avoiding Consequence

You cannot eliminate consequence.
You can manage it.

Ways to reduce impact include:
- Preparation
- Retreat
- Delegation
- Rest and recovery
- Accepting partial success
- Changing approach

Knowing when *not* to push is a form of mastery.

---

## Strain and Growth

Strain is not inherently negative.

Managed strain:
- Builds capacity
- Deepens skill
- Clarifies limits
- Reveals alignment

Unmanaged strain:
- Warps judgement
- Erodes coherence
- Leads to collapse

Growth occurs at the boundary — not beyond it.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- Actions feel consequential
- Failure remains meaningful
- Power has texture
- The world responds coherently
- Players learn through experience

There is no “safe grind”.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “Can I afford to fail?”

Ask instead:
- “What happens if I keep pushing?”
- “What am I carrying forward?”
- “What am I ignoring?”

The answers will arrive — whether you ask or not.
