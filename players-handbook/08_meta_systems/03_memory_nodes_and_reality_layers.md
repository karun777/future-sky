# Memory Nodes and Reality Layers

Not all places are equal.

Some locations in Future Sky remember more than they should. They accumulate resonance, preserve fragments of past states, and act as anchors for events that refuse to fully pass. These are memory nodes.

This chapter explains how memory nodes form, how reality layers overlap, and why certain places become pivotal across eras.

---

## What Is a Memory Node?

A memory node is a location, object, or moment that retains significance beyond its immediate context.

Memory nodes may:
- Preserve emotional or narrative residue
- Anchor echoes from other timelines
- Reactivate under pressure
- Alter how reality behaves locally
- Attract attention from powerful forces

A memory node is not frozen in time.
It is *charged*.

---

## How Memory Nodes Form

Memory nodes form when:
- An event carries extreme consequence
- A choice affects many lives
- A boundary is crossed
- A truth is revealed or buried
- A convergence occurs

Repetition strengthens nodes.
Denial destabilises them.

---

## Reality Layers

Reality in Future Sky is layered.

Layers may include:
- Physical reality
- Symbolic or mythic resonance
- Temporal echoes
- Psychological imprint
- Systemic distortion

Most of the time, layers align.
At memory nodes, they slip.

---

## Experiencing Layered Reality

At a memory node, characters may experience:
- Shifts in perception
- Inconsistent physics
- Emotional bleed-through
- Conflicting memories
- Altered cause and effect

These effects are not hallucinations.
They are information.

---

## Stability and Instability

Some memory nodes are stable.

Others are volatile.

Instability may manifest as:
- Temporal loops
- Repeating events
- Spatial distortion
- Emotional amplification
- System interference

Stability often depends on acknowledgment.

---

## Interaction and Activation

Memory nodes may be activated intentionally or accidentally.

Activation may require:
- Presence of specific individuals
- Particular timing
- Emotional resonance
- Ritual or action
- Technological or meta-system interaction

Once activated, a node responds.

---

## Memory Nodes and the Cube

Cubes interact strongly with memory nodes.

They may:
- Record additional data
- Unlock suppressed functions
- Reveal alternate layers
- Stabilise or destabilise a node

The Cube recognises what the world remembers.

---

## World Knowledge of Nodes

Different groups understand memory nodes differently.

Some:
- Guard them
- Exploit them
- Avoid them
- Build around them
- Misinterpret them entirely

Few understand the full implications.

---

## Healing, Scarring, and Erasure

Memory nodes can change.

They may:
- Heal through integration
- Scar through neglect
- Collapse into absence
- Be forcibly erased — at cost

Erasure removes memory from the world.
Something always fills the gap.

---

## Why This Matters

This system ensures that:
- Places carry history
- Exploration rewards attention
- The world feels layered
- Events leave physical trace
- Myth and mechanics align

Some locations are stories you can enter.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “Is this place dangerous?”

Ask instead:
- “What happened here?”
- “What hasn’t resolved?”
- “What does this place want remembered?”

The ground beneath you is listening.
