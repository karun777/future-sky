# IRL Nodes and QRs of Commotion

Future Sky is designed to remain fully playable without ever leaving the table or screen.

However, for groups who choose to engage more deeply, certain systems allow the world to gently extend into real life. These are IRL nodes — optional points where physical places, artefacts, or actions intersect with the game through symbolic or digital markers.

This chapter explains how IRL nodes work, what QRs of Commotion are, and how to engage safely and consensually.

---

## Optional by Design

IRL interaction is always optional.

You never need to:
- Visit a physical location
- Scan a code
- Attend an event
- Reveal personal information

Future Sky remains complete without IRL nodes.
They exist to add texture, not pressure.

---

## What Is an IRL Node?

An IRL node is a real-world anchor that connects to the game world symbolically or digitally.

Examples include:
- A physical location
- A shared object
- A public artwork
- A temporary installation
- A real-world event

IRL nodes mirror memory nodes in the game world.

---

## QRs of Commotion

QRs of Commotion are scannable markers that link IRL nodes to in-game effects.

A QR of Commotion may:
- Unlock information
- Trigger a narrative fragment
- Reveal a memory echo
- Update a Cube record
- Mark presence or discovery

They are not trackers.
They are keys.

---

## Symbolic, Not Literal

IRL nodes are symbolic extensions, not literal requirements.

Scanning a QR:
- Does not require precise location data
- Does not verify identity
- Does not confer competitive advantage
- Does not penalise non-participation

Meaning comes from recognition, not proof.

---

## Shared Discovery

IRL nodes may be:
- Discovered collaboratively
- Shared between players
- Encountered accidentally
- Engaged with asynchronously

Discovery often matters more than exclusivity.

---

## Safety and Consent

Engagement with IRL nodes should always:
- Respect personal boundaries
- Avoid private or restricted spaces
- Use public or clearly permitted locations
- Be opt-in and reversible

No game mechanic overrides real-world safety.

---

## Temporal and Narrative Effects

IRL node engagement may:
- Unlock narrative content
- Advance a thread
- Add resonance to a Cube
- Alter world mood subtly

Effects are additive, not mandatory.

---

## Portability and Substitution

IRL nodes are designed to be portable.

If a group cannot access a node:
- Substitutes may be provided
- Narrative equivalents may be unlocked
- Effects may occur through other means

The story adapts.

---

## Why This Matters

This system exists to:
- Blur boundaries playfully
- Anchor myth in lived space
- Encourage curiosity
- Support community storytelling
- Extend the world without enclosing players

The world leaks gently.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “Do I have to go there?”

Ask instead:
- “What does this place represent?”
- “What memory does it echo?”
- “How does the world mark being noticed?”

Reality is just another layer.
