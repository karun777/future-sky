# Meta Systems

Some forces in Future Sky operate above the level of immediate action.

They do not resolve in a turn, a scene, or even a single campaign arc. These are meta systems — structures that shape reality, memory, and consequence across time, space, and play modes.

This section introduces those systems and explains how they influence the world without becoming rules players must constantly track.

---

## What Is a Meta System?

A meta system is a persistent structure that:
- Operates across scenes and sessions
- Persists through time and eras
- Accumulates consequence slowly
- Influences multiple systems at once
- Cannot be solved by force alone

Meta systems create context rather than conflict.

---

## The Purpose of Meta Systems

Meta systems exist to:
- Create long-term tension
- Anchor narrative continuity
- Allow the world to remember
- Support asynchronous and transmedia play
- Give meaning to slow change

They reward patience, awareness, and interpretation.

---

## The Cube

Each character is bound to a Cube.

The Cube is:
- A personal vault
- A memory structure
- A navigational aid
- A record of progression
- A partially understood artefact

The Cube breaks rules quietly.

It remembers things the world forgets.

---

## Timelines and Echoes

Time in Future Sky is layered.

Actions may:
- Echo across eras
- Create parallel outcomes
- Leave dormant traces
- Re-emerge under pressure

Timelines do not overwrite each other cleanly.
They interfere.

---

## Memory Nodes and Reality Layers

Some places remember more than others.

Memory nodes are locations, objects, or moments that:
- Store resonance
- Preserve altered states
- Anchor narrative truth
- Allow re-entry or reinterpretation

Reality is not uniform everywhere.

---

## IRL Nodes and QRs of Commotion

Future Sky is porous.

Certain systems extend beyond the table or screen:
- Physical locations
- Scannable markers
- Real-world events
- Shared artefacts

These nodes blur the boundary between play and life — carefully, optionally, and with consent.

---

## Comets as Meta Pressure

Comets are not just astronomical events.

They represent:
- Irreversible approach
- Delayed consequence
- World-scale deadlines
- Collective urgency
- Mythic inevitability

A comet does not need to strike to matter.

Its presence alone reshapes behaviour.

---

## The Long Arc

Meta systems operate on the long arc.

They:
- Outlast characters
- Survive campaigns
- Shape history
- Define eras
- Become myth

Players may influence them.
They may not control them.

---

## Visibility and Mystery

Not all meta systems are fully explained.

Some are:
- Partially understood
- Fragmented across sources
- Known only through effect
- Interpreted differently by factions

Mystery is intentional.

---

## Why This Matters

This approach ensures that:
- The world feels vast
- Stakes scale beyond combat
- Long-term play remains meaningful
- Player actions echo
- Time itself becomes a character

Some systems are not meant to be solved.

They are meant to be lived with.

---

## A Final Orientation

Do not ask:
- “How do we stop this?”

Ask instead:
- “What does this change?”
- “Who responds first?”
- “What becomes inevitable?”

The comet is already in the sky.
