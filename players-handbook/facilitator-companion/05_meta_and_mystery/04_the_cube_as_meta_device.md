# The Cube as a Meta Device

The Cube is not just an artefact.

It is an interface.

Within the fiction, it is mysterious, ancient, and partially understood. Within the system, it is a boundary object — a place where story, memory, and mechanics touch without fully merging.

This chapter explains how to use the Cube as a meta device without collapsing its mystery.

---

## What the Cube Is (Structurally)

From a facilitator’s perspective, the Cube functions as:
- a persistent memory store
- a cross-era anchor
- a rule-bending permission space
- a container for hidden systems
- a narrative explanation for persistence

It exists so the game can *remember itself*.

---

## What the Cube Is Not

The Cube is not:
- a vending machine for rewards
- a shortcut for difficulty
- a universal solution
- a fully explained artefact

If it becomes any of these, it loses its power.

---

## The Cube as Boundary

The Cube sits at the edge of:
- character awareness
- player knowledge
- system structure

Things can pass through the Cube that cannot exist cleanly elsewhere.

Use it as a liminal space.

---

## When to Invoke the Cube

Invoke the Cube when:
- continuity matters across eras
- memory needs to persist
- a system exception feels earned
- players are ready for deeper layers

Do not invoke it early or casually.

---

## Cube Interactions Are Incomplete

Every interaction with the Cube should feel:
- partial
- provisional
- constrained

Players should leave with:
- more questions than answers
- a sense of depth
- new possibilities, not closure

---

## Unlocking Without Explaining

Cube unlocks may include:
- new interfaces
- altered rules
- recovered memory fragments
- expanded capacity
- strange permissions

Do not explain how or why these unlock.

Let usage precede understanding.

---

## The Cube and Trust

The Cube should never:
- invalidate player choice
- erase consequence
- undo earned outcomes

If the Cube intervenes, it should:
- complicate
- transform
- preserve at a cost

Trust is more important than surprise.

---

## Using the Cube to Hold Contradiction

The Cube can contain:
- conflicting truths
- incompatible timelines
- paradoxical data

You do not need to resolve these contradictions.
The Cube can hold them safely.

---

## The Cube as a Facilitator Tool

For facilitators, the Cube allows you to:
- justify persistence cleanly
- introduce meta-elements gently
- align tabletop and digital state
- seed long-term mystery

Use it to support play — not to control it.

---

## Scaling Cube Presence

Early play:
- the Cube is distant
- rarely accessed
- poorly understood

Later play:
- deeper access
- stranger behaviour
- higher stakes

Let presence grow with readiness.

---

## A Facilitator Prompt

Before using the Cube, ask:
> “Does this deepen continuity, or does it shortcut consequence?”

If it shortcuts, don’t use it.

---

## Final Orientation

The Cube exists so the game can hold more than it can explain.

Treat it with restraint.

If players sense that the Cube knows more than it reveals — and that interacting with it carries weight — the device is working.
