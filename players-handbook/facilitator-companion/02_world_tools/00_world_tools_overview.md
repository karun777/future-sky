# World Tools: Overview

World tools are how *Future Sky* stays alive between player actions.

They allow you to model pressure, memory, and change without scripting outcomes or micromanaging detail. Used well, they let the world act with coherence — even when players are elsewhere.

This section introduces the primary tools you will use to shape the world over time.

---

## Think in Systems, Not Scenes

Scenes are moments.
World tools are continuities.

Rather than preparing what will happen, you prepare:
- what is under strain
- who wants what
- what is unstable
- what is approaching

The world then responds naturally to player interaction.

---

## What World Tools Do

World tools help you:
- track unresolved tensions
- escalate stakes gradually
- reflect player impact at scale
- generate events without railroading
- maintain coherence across sessions

They reduce improvisational load by giving you *anchors*.

---

## The Core World Tools

This section focuses on four primary tools:

- **Threads** — persistent narrative tensions  
- **Factions** — organised actors with memory  
- **Ambient Mood** — background emotional and environmental tone  
- **Pressure & Events** — escalation and release mechanisms  

Each tool can be used lightly or deeply.

---

## Threads: Ongoing Tension

Threads represent unresolved forces in the world.

A thread might be:
- a looming disaster
- a fragile alliance
- a spreading rumour
- an unstable location

Threads advance when ignored, stressed, or triggered.

You do not need many threads.
A few well-tended ones go far.

---

## Factions: The World Acts Back

Factions are not quest-givers.

They are:
- interest-driven
- reactive
- imperfectly informed
- capable of initiative

Factions give the world momentum beyond the players.

---

## Ambient Mood: The Quiet Signal

Not all change is loud.

Ambient mood reflects:
- emotional atmosphere
- environmental stability
- background stress

Mood cues help players *feel* the world shifting before consequences arrive.

---

## Pressure and Events: Timing Matters

Pressure tools allow you to:
- delay consequences without negating them
- compress time meaningfully
- create windows of urgency

Events emerge when pressure crosses thresholds.

---

## Light Touch, Heavy Impact

You do not need to track everything.

World tools work best when:
- simple
- visible to you
- occasionally visible to players

Over-detail kills flexibility.
Under-detail kills coherence.

---

## Tools Are Optional, Not Mandatory

You can:
- use only threads
- ignore factions for a while
- abstract mood entirely
- trigger events manually

World tools are supports, not obligations.

---

## Between Sessions

World tools shine between sessions.

After a session, ask:
- Which threads advanced?
- Which factions reacted?
- What pressure increased or released?
- What mood changed?

Answering these takes minutes, not hours.

---

## A Facilitator Mindset Shift

Instead of asking:
- “What happens next?”

Ask:
- “What is becoming unstable?”
- “Who will act if nothing changes?”
- “What is ripening for consequence?”

The world will answer you.

---

## A Final Orientation

World tools do not replace imagination.

They focus it.

Used well, they allow *Future Sky* to feel responsive, alive, and continuous — without ever needing a script.
