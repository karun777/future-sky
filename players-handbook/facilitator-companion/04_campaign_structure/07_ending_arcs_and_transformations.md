# Ending Arcs and Transformations

Endings in Future Sky are not conclusions.

They are moments when the shape of the world has changed enough that the current story no longer applies.

This chapter helps you end arcs — and entire campaigns — in a way that honours what came before without freezing the future.

---

## What an Ending Is

An ending is not:
- total resolution
- universal victory
- narrative closure

An ending is a **new equilibrium**.

Something that could not exist before now exists.
Something that once mattered no longer does.

---

## When an Arc Is Ready to End

An arc is ready to end when:
- its central tension has resolved
- its driving question has been answered
- further escalation would feel redundant

Dragging an arc past this point weakens its impact.

---

## Signs a Campaign Is Ending

A campaign may be nearing its end when:
- major factions have transformed or collapsed
- the core pressures have dissipated or crystallised
- players begin asking “what comes next?” instead of “what do we do now?”

Listen to that shift.
It is important.

---

## Endings Through Transformation

Good endings change:
- the political landscape
- the technological baseline
- the cultural norms
- the environmental conditions
- the metaphysical rules

The world should feel recognisably different.

---

## Character Endings

Characters do not need:
- perfect resolution
- completed arcs
- happy outcomes

They need:
- meaningful change
- earned position
- acknowledged impact

Leave space for interpretation.

---

## Naming the Change

At the end of an arc or campaign:
- name what has changed
- name what was lost
- name what remains unresolved

This anchors memory.

---

## Letting Threads Fall Silent

Some threads do not end.

They fade.

Letting a question remain unanswered can be more powerful than resolving it artificially.

---

## Avoiding Over-Explanation

Do not explain everything.

Trust players to:
- remember moments
- connect themes
- carry meaning forward

Silence can be part of the ending.

---

## Closing Without Finality

Future Sky worlds are never “finished.”

Endings should feel like:
- a door closing
- a horizon shifting
- a sky changing colour

Not a hard stop.

---

## Using Endings as Seeds

An ending may:
- become history in a future campaign
- echo in another era
- distort timelines elsewhere
- live on through memory nodes or artefacts

Nothing is wasted.

---

## Facilitator Closure Rituals

Consider ending with:
- a final montage
- a shared reflection
- a last question posed to the table
- a symbolic gesture or image

Ritual helps transition.

---

## A Facilitator Prompt

At the end, ask:
> “What could never go back to the way it was?”

Sit with the answer.

---

## Final Orientation

Endings are acts of care.

If players leave feeling that something *mattered* — even if it was unfinished — the transformation was real.

The sky has changed.
