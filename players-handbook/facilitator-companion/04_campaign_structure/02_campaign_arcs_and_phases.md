# Campaign Arcs and Phases

A Future Sky campaign does not move in a straight line.

It unfolds in phases — periods of relative stability, rising pressure, disruption, and transformation. Understanding these phases helps you pace escalation without forcing outcomes or rushing resolution.

---

## What an Arc Is

An arc is not a plotline.

An arc is a **pattern of change**:
- pressure builds
- choices compound
- the world shifts
- a new state emerges

Arcs are visible in hindsight more than in planning.

---

## Campaigns Move in Phases

Most campaigns cycle through these phases:

1. **Establishment**
2. **Destabilisation**
3. **Escalation**
4. **Crisis**
5. **Aftermath**
6. **Transformation**

Not every arc uses every phase — but most touch several.

---

## Phase 1: Establishment

In this phase:
- the status quo is legible
- pressures exist but are contained
- factions operate within known bounds

Players learn:
- how the world works
- who holds power
- what feels normal

This phase grounds later disruption.

---

## Phase 2: Destabilisation

Destabilisation begins when:
- a balance is disturbed
- a hidden force emerges
- a key resource becomes contested
- player action introduces friction

Signals appear before explosions.
Mood shifts, rumours spread, systems strain.

---

## Phase 3: Escalation

Escalation is marked by:
- faster consequences
- clearer sides
- rising stakes
- shrinking margins for error

During escalation:
- inaction becomes a choice
- neutral positions erode
- pressure accelerates

This is where campaigns often become intense.

---

## Phase 4: Crisis

Crisis is when:
- time hardens
- outcomes lock in
- consequences cascade quickly

Crisis does not mean final battle.
It means the situation can no longer remain unresolved.

Players should feel:
- urgency
- weight
- commitment

---

## Phase 5: Aftermath

After crisis:
- systems settle
- losses are counted
- gains are secured
- narratives spread

This phase allows:
- reflection
- healing
- reorientation

Do not skip aftermath.
It gives meaning to what came before.

---

## Phase 6: Transformation

Transformation is the new normal.

After an arc:
- factions have shifted
- environments have changed
- characters have evolved

This is not a reset.
It is a new baseline.

Future arcs grow from here.

---

## Arcs Can Overlap

Campaigns often contain:
- multiple arcs at once
- arcs at different phases
- background arcs advancing quietly

Not everything peaks at the same time.
That texture makes the world feel alive.

---

## Player-Driven Arcs

Some arcs are created entirely by player action.

If players:
- seize unexpected power
- dismantle a key institution
- forge a lasting alliance

An arc has begun — even if you did not plan it.

Follow the change.

---

## Ending an Arc

An arc ends when:
- its central tension resolves
- the world stabilises
- a new question takes its place

Do not drag arcs past their natural end.
Closure creates momentum for what follows.

---

## Facilitator Guidance

You do not need to label phases at the table.

Use them internally to:
- sense when to escalate
- know when to slow down
- recognise when something has shifted

Trust the rhythm.

---

## Final Orientation

Campaign arcs are not rails.

They are currents.

If players feel carried forward by consequence rather than pushed by plot, the arc is working.
