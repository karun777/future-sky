# Cross-Era Play and Parallelism

Future Sky is not set in a single time.

It exists across multiple eras that are distinct, connected, and reflective of one another. These eras are not alternate campaigns — they are parallel expressions of the same underlying tensions.

This chapter explains how to run cross-era play without confusion, contradiction, or loss of coherence.

---

## What Parallel Eras Are

The eras of Future Sky are:
- **Jyotzon** — the mythic, ancestral future
- **Omnicron** — the collapsing present
- **Manzo** — the far-frontier aftermath

They are not separate timelines in the traditional sense.

They are different **contextual expressions of the same evolutionary pressures**.

---

## Parallelism, Not Causality

Cross-era play is not primarily about cause and effect.

It is about **resonance**.

Actions in one era may:
- echo symbolically
- mirror structurally
- reveal hidden patterns
- unlock understanding elsewhere

Avoid strict “if X then Y” rules.
Let meaning travel before mechanics.

---

## Shared Problems, Different Forms

Each era faces similar challenges:
- scarcity
- power imbalance
- technological overreach
- cultural fracture
- ecological instability

They appear differently depending on context.

Recognising these parallels is part of play.

---

## Characters Across Eras

Characters may:
- incarnate differently
- echo archetypes
- inherit unresolved tensions
- carry memory fragments

A character is not required to exist in all eras — but the *themes they embody* may.

---

## Facilitating Cross-Era Shifts

Shifts between eras should:
- be intentional
- be signposted
- feel meaningful

Use:
- dreams
- artefacts
- memory nodes
- Cube interactions
- world events

Do not shift eras casually.
Treat it as a change in gravity.

---

## Maintaining Coherence

To avoid confusion:
- keep each era internally consistent
- reuse symbols, names, or locations sparingly
- highlight thematic parallels, not mechanical sameness

Let players orient themselves emotionally before mechanically.

---

## Player Choice Across Eras

Players may:
- focus on one era
- move between eras episodically
- experience echoes without direct control

All are valid.

Do not force cross-era play.
Invite it.

---

## When Eras Interact Directly

Direct interaction between eras should be rare.

When it happens:
- mark it as exceptional
- let consequences be strange
- resist full explanation

Mystery is a feature, not a bug.

---

## Parallel Arcs

Campaign arcs may:
- resolve differently in each era
- complete in one era and destabilise another
- remain unresolved across all

Resolution does not require uniformity.

---

## The Role of the Facilitator

As facilitator, your task is not to reconcile timelines.

It is to:
- notice patterns
- highlight resonance
- honour contradiction

Trust players to feel meaning even without full clarity.

---

## A Facilitator Prompt

When moving between eras, ask:
> “What is the same problem wearing a different mask?”

Let that guide framing.

---

## Final Orientation

Cross-era play is not about mastery.

It is about perspective.

If players begin to sense that history, future, and myth are speaking to one another — even imperfectly — the parallelism is working.
