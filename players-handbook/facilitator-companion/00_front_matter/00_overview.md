# Facilitator Companion: Overview

This book is for those who hold the space.

The Facilitator Companion exists to support the person (or people) guiding play in *Future Sky* — not as an authority figure, but as a steward of tone, pressure, and consequence.

If the Player’s Handbook explains *what characters can do*, this book focuses on *how the world responds*.

---

## What This Book Is

This is not a rulebook extension.

It is:
- A practical guide to running the game
- A toolkit for improvisation under pressure
- A shared language for adjudicating consequence
- A lens for seeing the system as a living whole

It assumes you have read the Player’s Handbook, but it does not require mastery of it.

---

## The Role of the Facilitator

In *Future Sky*, the Facilitator is not:
- A narrator performing a story
- An adversary trying to defeat players
- A referee enforcing rules

The Facilitator *is*:
- A signal reader
- A pressure modulator
- A consequence translator
- A keeper of continuity

Your primary job is not to decide outcomes — it is to decide *what the world cares about*.

---

## A Living World, Not a Script

Future Sky does not expect you to prepare plots.

Instead, you will:
- Track tensions
- Observe player priorities
- Let systems accumulate weight
- Release pressure at meaningful moments

Preparation focuses on *conditions*, not *scenes*.

---

## Tone First, Mechanics Second

Tone is the invisible system that governs all others.

This book will help you:
- Establish tone early
- Reinforce it through consequence
- Shift it deliberately when needed
- Protect it when systems collide

If a mechanical ruling breaks tone, tone wins.

---

## Pressure as a Design Tool

Pressure is what turns choice into agency.

You will learn how to:
- Open and close pressure windows
- Escalate stakes without railroading
- Allow failure to matter without stalling play
- Use time as a narrative lever

Pressure should clarify decisions, not punish them.

---

## Transparency Over Mystery

Facilitators are encouraged to:
- Explain why rulings are made
- Surface stakes before rolls
- Share how systems are responding
- Invite player interpretation

Mystery belongs in the world — not in the rules.

---

## Modular Authority

You are empowered to:
- Add optional modules
- Remove unnecessary complexity
- Adjust pacing mid-campaign
- Shape play culture at your table

Consistency matters more than correctness.

---

## This Book Is Not a Performance Test

You do not need:
- Voices
- Encyclopedic lore knowledge
- Perfect improvisation
- Total preparation

You need:
- Attention
- Curiosity
- Willingness to pause
- Respect for player intent

Everything else grows with use.

---

## How to Use This Book

Read lightly.
Mark pages.
Steal phrasing.
Adapt tools.

This book is meant to be written *on*, not read *through*.

---

## A Final Orientation

If you ever feel unsure, return to three questions:

- What is under pressure right now?
- What would make this choice meaningful?
- What will the world remember?

Answer those honestly.

The rest will follow.
