# Facilitator Toolkit Overview

This section is not theory.

It is not philosophy.
It is not lore.
It is not exhaustive.

This section exists to help you **run the game at the table**.

The Toolkit provides practical structures, scripts, templates, and generators you can use:
- before a session
- during play
- when improvising under pressure
- when the game surprises you

You are not meant to use everything.
You are meant to use what you need.

---

## What This Toolkit Is For

The Toolkit helps you:
- start sessions cleanly
- frame scenes quickly
- escalate tension without railroading
- resolve uncertainty with confidence
- generate content without prep overload

It is designed for *live use*.

---

## How to Use This Section

You can:
- read it front to back once
- bookmark your favourite templates
- print key pages
- keep it open on a second screen

Each tool is:
- optional
- modular
- compatible with improvisation

Nothing here overrides your judgment.

---

## Philosophy of the Toolkit

The tools in this section follow three principles:

1. **Clarity beats cleverness**  
   If a tool is confusing, discard it.

2. **Pressure creates meaning**  
   Most tools introduce choice under constraint.

3. **Structure enables freedom**  
   Templates are scaffolding, not scripts.

---

## Scripts Are Starting Points

Some tools are written as scripts.

These are not meant to be performed verbatim unless that feels right.
They exist to:
- give language when you need it
- reduce cognitive load
- support consistency

Make them your own.

---

## Templates Invite Variation

Templates define:
- shape
- flow
- pressure points

They do not define:
- outcomes
- themes
- solutions

Players fill those in.

---

## Generators Are Prompts, Not Answers

Tables and generators exist to:
- spark ideas
- break creative blocks
- add texture

Roll once.
Roll twice.
Ignore the result if something better emerges.

---

## The Toolkit and the System

Nothing in this section:
- replaces the rules
- contradicts core mechanics
- forces outcomes

These tools work *with* the system, not against it.

---

## Final Orientation

If you ever feel stuck, overwhelmed, or unsure:
- pick one tool
- apply it lightly
- keep play moving

Momentum matters more than perfection.

This section exists so you don’t have to carry the whole world in your head.
