# Session Zero Script

Session Zero is not about rules.

It is about alignment.

This script helps you establish shared expectations, tone, consent, and trust before play begins. You may run it in full, in part, or adapt it freely.

What matters is not the words — it is the clarity they create.

---

## Opening the Session

You may begin with something like:

> “Before we start, I want to make sure we’re aligned on the kind of game we’re playing, how we’ll treat each other, and what this space is for.”

Pause.
Let the table settle.

---

## What Kind of Game Is This?

Say, in your own words:

> “Future Sky is a game about choice, consequence, and change.  
> It’s not about winning.  
> It’s about how your characters respond to pressure over time.”

Invite questions.

---

## Player Agency

Clarify:

> “Your choices matter.  
> I won’t railroad outcomes.  
> The world will respond to what you do — even if that response is uncomfortable.”

Emphasise:
- consequences are real
- failure is meaningful
- success may change the world

---

## Tone and Boundaries

Say:

> “This game can include tension, danger, and difficult themes — but we decide together what is okay at this table.”

Discuss:
- tone (hopeful, dark, surreal, grounded, etc.)
- content boundaries
- off-limits topics
- safety tools in use

Make space for quiet voices.

---

## Safety and Consent

Establish clearly:
- players may step away at any time
- players may pause or redirect content
- consent can be withdrawn without explanation

Reassure:
> “No one needs to justify their limits.”

---

## Meta and Table Talk

Explain:

> “We’ll sometimes talk out of character.  
> That’s normal.  
> We’ll just make sure important decisions are grounded in the fiction.”

No policing.
Just awareness.

---

## How Rules Will Be Used

Set expectations:

> “I’ll aim for consistency, clarity, and fairness.  
> If something isn’t covered, I’ll make a ruling and we’ll keep moving.”

Add:
> “We can revisit things between sessions.”

---

## The Role of the Facilitator

State plainly:

> “I’m not here to defeat you.  
> I’m here to present a world that responds honestly to your actions.”

This matters more than mechanics.

---

## Player Responsibilities

Invite agreement on:
- respecting each other’s time
- staying engaged
- sharing spotlight
- communicating needs

Keep it light, not contractual.

---

## Characters and Themes

Ask players:
- what kind of character they want to explore
- what themes interest them
- what arcs excite them

Listen more than you speak.

---

## The Long View

Explain:

> “This game unfolds over time.  
> Things will change.  
> Characters may grow, fracture, or transform.”

Set expectation of evolution.

---

## Closing the Session Zero

End with something like:

> “If anything comes up during play — confusion, discomfort, excitement — we can talk about it. This is a shared space.”

Then transition:
- into character creation
- or into the opening scene

---

## Facilitator Notes

- You do not need to rush this.
- Silence is not failure.
- Alignment now saves repair later.

---

## Final Orientation

A good Session Zero doesn’t feel like a meeting.

It feels like a door opening.

If players leave feeling heard, curious, and safe, you have already succeeded.
