# How to Use This Book

This book is not meant to be read once, cover to cover.

It is meant to be **returned to**.

Future Sky is a layered system: narrative, mechanics, and world logic are intertwined. This book reflects that structure. Some sections explain *how things feel*. Others explain *how things work*. Both are necessary.

---

## Who This Book Is For

This book is written for:
- Players new to Future Sky
- Facilitators, moderators, or game runners
- Tabletop players adapting the system to other formats
- Developers and designers working within the Future Sky framework

You do not need to understand everything before you begin playing.
You are not expected to memorise rules.
Understanding will accumulate through use.

---

## Reading Order (Suggested, Not Required)

If you are new to Future Sky:
1. Read the **Introduction** section in full
2. Skim **Character Creation**
3. Read **Core Concepts** as questions arise
4. Return to **Rules** when needed

If you are facilitating or running a game:
- Read **Core Concepts**
- Familiarise yourself with **Time & Events**
- Reference **Combat** and **World Interaction** as required

If you are adapting Future Sky to another format:
- Focus on **Rules Engine**
- Read **Design Philosophy** in the appendices
- Treat narrative sections as guidance, not constraint

---

## Modular by Design

Each section of this book is designed to stand on its own.

You may:
- Read chapters out of order
- Ignore systems not relevant to your style of play
- Modify rules to suit your group

Future Sky is resilient to alteration.
If a change breaks something, it will usually break *loudly*, not silently.

That is intentional.

---

## Provisional Rules and Evolving Systems

Some rules in this book are marked as:
- *Provisional*
- *Subject to playtesting*
- *Context-dependent*

These markers are not warnings.
They are invitations.

Future Sky is a living system. The rules are expected to evolve as the world and its players evolve.

When in doubt:
- Favour consistency
- Favour clarity
- Favour meaning over precision

---

## Dice, Interpretation, and Intent

Dice are used throughout this system.

They introduce uncertainty, not authority.

A roll should never override:
- Clear intent
- Established fiction
- Player consent

When the rules and the story appear to conflict, pause.
Clarify what is actually at stake.
Then decide which outcome creates the most honest result.

---

## Tabletop and Digital Play

Future Sky was designed to exist across:
- Digital platforms
- Tabletop sessions
- Asynchronous play
- Live, time-sensitive events

Not all systems will apply in all contexts.

This book provides a **shared language**, not a rigid engine.
Use what serves the experience you are creating.

---

## When to Ask Questions

If something is unclear, that is not a failure.

Future Sky assumes:
- Questions will arise
- Interpretations will differ
- Groups will resolve ambiguity differently

Write your decisions down.
Return to them later.
Let the system remember.

---

## A Closing Thought

This book is not a map.
It is a **set of instruments**.

How they are played will determine the music.

Take your time.
Read when needed.
Act when it feels right.

The world will respond.
