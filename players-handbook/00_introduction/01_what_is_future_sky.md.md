# What Is Future Sky?

Future Sky is a roleplaying game about **movement through time, memory, and meaning**.

It is not a game about winning.
It is not a game about clearing dungeons.
It is not a game about perfect balance.

Future Sky is a game about **becoming someone inside a living system** — a world that remembers what you do, changes because of it, and sometimes resists you in return.

Players do not arrive as heroes.
They arrive as *people*, displaced from somewhere else, carrying fragments of identity they may not yet understand.

---

## A World Across Timelines

The world of Future Sky exists across **multiple eras** — ancient, collapsing, and far-future — which echo and interfere with one another.

Actions taken in one time may ripple forward or backward.
Memories may survive bodies.
Places may exist in several forms at once.

Time is not a straight line.
It is a **woven field**.

Players are not outside this system.
They are part of it.

---

## Narrative First, Rules With Purpose

Future Sky is **narrative-driven**, but it is not freeform.

Rules exist to:
- Give weight to choice
- Create tension and consequence
- Allow the world to respond consistently
- Let stories emerge rather than be scripted

The rules do not tell you *what story to tell*.
They tell you **how the world pushes back** when you act within it.

Dice are not arbiters of success.
They are instruments of uncertainty.

---

## A Living Game World

The world of Future Sky continues whether you are present or not.

- Time usually flows softly, asynchronously.
- Sometimes it tightens.
- Occasionally, it becomes urgent.

There may be moments when hesitation costs you.
There may be long stretches where reflection is rewarded.

Both states are intentional.

Future Sky is designed to support:
- Solo play
- Group play
- Asynchronous participation
- Live, high-intensity moments

No single pace is “correct.”

---

## Character as Resonance

Your character in Future Sky is not just a collection of statistics.

They are a **pattern of resonance**:
- Across chakras
- Across elements
- Across memory
- Across fate

Attributes, skills, and abilities reflect *how* your character engages with the world, not merely how strong or clever they are.

Growth is not only vertical.
It is lateral.
Sometimes it is internal.

Who you become matters as much as what you can do.

---

## Combat Exists — But It Is Not the Center

Conflict exists in Future Sky.
So does violence.

But combat is **one expression of tension**, not the default solution.

Avoidance, negotiation, preparation, insight, timing, and sacrifice are often more powerful than force.

When combat does occur, it is:
- Consequential
- Sometimes lethal
- Rarely trivial

Survival is never guaranteed.
Meaning often is.

---

## The Player’s Role

As a player, you are not expected to master the system immediately.

You are expected to:
- Pay attention
- Make choices
- Accept uncertainty
- Live with outcomes

The world will meet you where you are.
It will not always protect you.

That is part of the pact.

---

## What Future Sky Is Not

Future Sky is not:
- A power fantasy
- A loot treadmill
- A perfectly fair simulation
- A solved puzzle

It is not designed to reward optimisation above all else.
It is designed to reward **engagement, presence, and curiosity**.

---

## An Evolving Text

This book is not final.
The world is not finished.
The rules will grow, fracture, and refine.

That is not a flaw.
It is the nature of the system.

Future Sky is a game that remembers.

And once you enter it,
**it remembers you.**
